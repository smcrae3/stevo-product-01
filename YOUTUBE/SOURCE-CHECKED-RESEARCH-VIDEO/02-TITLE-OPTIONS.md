# 02 — Title Options

No fake-income clickbait. All five keep the actual premise: AI discovers,
source proves, human approves.

1. **How I Use AI for Research Without Hallucinating**
2. **AI Found the Data — But I Didn't Trust It Yet**
3. **The AI Research Rule That Stops Bad Data**
4. **Don't Let AI Guess — Here's What I Do Instead**
5. **AI Research, But Every Fact Has to Prove Itself**

## Recommended: #1 — "How I Use AI for Research Without Hallucinating"

Reasoning: leads with practical value ("how I use AI") rather than a
gotcha, uses a real term people search for ("hallucinating") that signals
the video answers a genuine, current concern, and doesn't require the
viewer to already know the water-utility example to understand what the
video's about.

# Automation Audit — Sample Report
### Prepared for: "Riverside Home Services" *(hypothetical example business)*
### Format: this is what a buyer receives for the $99 Automation Audit

---

## 1. Scope of this audit
A 60–90 minute review of the business's current lead intake and follow-up
process (as described by the owner, or observed in shared screenshots/
access), producing this written report: what's working, what's leaking
time or leads, and a short list of fixes ranked by effort vs. impact. No
changes are made during the audit itself — that's the point of keeping it
at $99: low commitment, clear deliverable, no surprises.

## 2. Current process (as described)
- New leads arrive via a Google Form embedded on the business's website.
- Form submissions land in a Google Sheet. No one is notified in real time.
- The owner checks the sheet "when I remember," typically once a day.
- Follow-up is a manual phone call or text, timing depends on the day.

## 3. Findings

| # | Finding | Impact | Effort to fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No notification when a form is submitted — leads sit unseen for hours | High (delayed response is the #1 driver of lost leads in service businesses) | Low |
| 2 | No standard first-response message — replies are improvised per lead | Medium (inconsistent impression, slower typing under time pressure) | Low |
| 3 | No log of who was contacted, when, or what was said | Medium (no way to know which leads went cold, no data to improve the process) | Low–Medium |
| 4 | No routing by job type — an emergency request waits in the same queue as a general inquiry | Medium | Medium |

## 4. Recommended fix, ranked by effort vs. impact

1. **(Do first)** Connect the Google Form to an instant notification (email
   or SMS) the moment a lead comes in, via Make.com. Effort: under an hour.
   Impact: closes the single biggest gap found.
2. **(Do second)** Add an automatic first-response email/text acknowledging
   the lead within seconds, so the buyer isn't waiting in silence while the
   owner is on a job. Effort: low. Impact: high — this is the mechanism
   behind the well-documented "5-minute response converts far better than
   next-day response" pattern in service businesses.
3. **(Do third)** Log every lead and every response automatically in the
   same Sheet, with a timestamp, so nothing is tracked by memory.
4. **(Optional, only if volume justifies it)** Route by job type/urgency
   keyword so emergencies surface first.

## 5. What this report does *not* include
This audit identifies and prioritizes the fixes. It does not build them.
Item 1–3 above is, together, the scope of the $199 Trades Lead Response
System (or a quoted bounded fix for a single item). This audit is
intentionally sized to answer "is this worth fixing, and what exactly needs
to happen" — not to be the implementation itself.

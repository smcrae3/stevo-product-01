# Phase 5 — Proof-to-Buyer Matching

## Inventory of existing proof assets: none

Per Phase 1, the workspace contains zero proof assets — no case studies, no
screenshots, no completed builds, no testimonials, no portfolio pieces of
any kind. This is expected and honest for a pre-revenue business (0
customers, $0 revenue) — there is no real client result to show yet, and
none should be invented. Nothing here claims otherwise.

## Matching (what would help, mapped to what's missing)

| Market problem / opportunity | Strongest existing proof | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer unsure what a "workflow automation audit" even produces | None | Needs to see the *shape* of the deliverable before paying $99 for it |
| Buyer with a broken Google Form → Sheet → Gmail flow (the exact bounded-fix scenario Phase 4 identifies as Upwork's best fit) | None | Needs to see that this exact kind of build is something Steven can actually architect, not just claim |
| Trades buyer evaluating the $199 Lead Response System (warm-intro channel) | None | Needs a concrete technical picture of what they're buying, since there's no case study to point to yet |

## Smallest missing proof asset worth building now

Two small, honestly-labeled artifacts were created in this folder — both
are genuine technical work product Steven could actually screenshot, attach
to a proposal, or use as the literal starting spec for a real build. Neither
claims a client, a result, a deployment, or an outcome that hasn't happened:

1. **`sample-automation-audit-report.md`** — a filled example of what a $99
   Audit deliverable looks like, using a clearly labeled hypothetical
   business (not a real client). Demonstrates the audit's structure, depth,
   and professionalism before a buyer commits $99 to find out.
2. **`trades-lead-response-blueprint.md`** — the actual technical
   architecture (trigger → routing → auto-response → logging → follow-up)
   for the $199 Trades Lead Response System, written at real build-spec
   depth. This is genuine IP, usable as-is on a real job, not a mockup.

**What was deliberately NOT built:** a large portfolio, multiple fake case
studies, a demo video, a fake before/after — none of that is warranted at
this stage and would be exactly the "busywork" the mission explicitly
warned against. Two artifacts, both truthful, both reusable in a real
proposal tomorrow, is the right size for where this business actually is.

## Labeling requirement (non-negotiable)

Both artifacts are headed with an explicit **"SAMPLE / ILLUSTRATIVE — not an
actual client engagement"** notice. Never remove that label or present
either artifact as a real client's results. That would cross directly into
the fabrication the operating rules prohibit.

## Verified general demand findings

**Trades/home-service lead-response automation — real, quantified, current pain.**
- 62% of inbound calls to US service businesses go unanswered; contractors
  miss 40–60% of calls while on job sites or driving.
- Leads contacted within 5 minutes convert roughly 8x better than leads
  contacted between 5 minutes and 24 hours later.
- Average annual cost of missed calls for a small contracting business is
  estimated at $45,000–$120,000.
- AI/automation adoption among home-service professionals has reached ~72%,
  with demand for these tools projected to grow ~21%/year through 2030.
- Source: [InstantBusinessPro, "The Real Cost of Missed Calls for Contractors in 2026"](https://instantbusinesspro.ai/post/cost-of-missed-calls);
  [Bullseye Marketing Consultants, "Improving Contractor Lead Response Time with AI"](https://www.bullseyemarketingconsultants.com/marketing/improving-contractor-lead-response-time-with-ai-the-2026-speed-to-lead-strategy/).

**Make.com / small-business workflow automation — active, broadening category.**
- Make.com has expanded past simple task automation into AI-augmented
  orchestration with 3,000+ integrations, explicitly marketed at SMBs
  automating lead management, not just enterprises.
- Upwork's own "AI Automation" job cluster spans several buyer phrasings
  (connecting AI to existing tools, building agents that take action,
  automating specific repetitive processes like lead qualification) — buyers
  don't all use the same keyword, so search terms need to be broad
  (automation, workflow, Zapier/Make, lead follow-up, form-to-sheet,
  Gmail automation), not narrowly literal.
- Source: [remoteaitools.com, "Upwork AI Automation Jobs 2026: Real Demand & Rates"](https://remoteaitools.com/upwork-ai-automation-jobs-2026/);
  [DEV Community, "Make.com 2026"](https://dev.to/dr_hernani_costa/makecom-2026-ai-agents-visual-workflow-automation-3972).

**Who is actually posting these jobs on Upwork.**
- Visible evidence points to digitally-native SMB owners and agencies as the
  more typical Upwork job-poster for automation work (e.g., a land brokerage
  business owner, marketing agencies hiring automation support), not
  brick-and-mortar trades businesses directly.
- **[INFERENCE, carried from Phase 2]**: this means Upwork is likely a
  *better-fitted channel for the Automation Audit and custom automation
  offers* (which target any digitally-operating small business) than for the
  Trades Lead Response System specifically (whose buyers — plumbers,
  electricians, HVAC owners — are less likely to be the ones posting
  developer jobs on a freelance marketplace). See Phase 4 for the resulting
  offer-to-channel recommendation.

**First-client mechanics that affect which jobs to even bid on.**
- Smaller fixed-price scopes ($100–$500) and first-time Upwork clients are
  both meaningfully more open to hiring an unreviewed freelancer than
  established, high-volume buyers are.
- Source: [Zenlance, "Upwork First Client With No Reviews"](https://zenlance.net/upwork-first-client-with-no-reviews/);
  [FreelanceFilter](https://www.freelancefilter.com/blog/why-upwork-proposals-get-ignored-winning-strategy-2026).

## Opportunity scoring rubric (use this on live Upwork listings tonight/tomorrow)

Since only Steven's logged-in Upwork session can see current live postings,
this rubric replaces the per-opportunity table the mission asked for. Score
each real job post Steven finds against this before bidding — 2 minutes per
listing:

| Signal | Good sign (+) | Bad sign (−) |
|---|---|---|
| Posted | < 2 hours ago | > 24 hours ago, or "Interviewing" already |
| Client history | First job posted, or < 5 hires | 50+ hires, enterprise-scale |
| Scope | Fixed-price $100–$500, clearly bounded | Vague, open-ended, "ongoing," or huge |
| Buyer's own words | Names a specific broken workflow/tool (e.g. "leads sit in my inbox," "form doesn't email me") | Generic "need automation expert" with no specifics |
| Stack named | Make.com, Zapier, Google Forms/Sheets, Gmail | Requires deep custom CRM/backend/telephony engineering outside demonstrated capability |
| Fit to offer | Matches $99 Audit (diagnosis-only ask) or $199/custom (a specific broken flow to fix) | Requires an ongoing retainer, enterprise SLA, or capabilities not demonstrated |

**Decision rule:** 4+ green signals → BID same day, ideally same hour.
2–3 green signals → POSSIBLE, bid if under weekly proposal capacity.
0–1 green signals, or any capability-gap red flag → SKIP, don't spend a
Connect proving a point.

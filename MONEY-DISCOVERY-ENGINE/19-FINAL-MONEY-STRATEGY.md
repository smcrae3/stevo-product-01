# 19 — FINAL MONEY STRATEGY (PHASE 21)

## WHAT SHOULD STEVEN DO TO MAKE MONEY NOW?

**Get paid by the hour for his judgment, immediately, on platforms that
require no customer acquisition — and use that income to fund the slower
build of an agency subcontracting channel that becomes an actual business.**

**Stop selling fixed-price research to strangers on Upwork with 27 Connects.**

---

## THE FIVE PLAYS, PRIORITIZED

### PRIMARY MONEY PLAY — AI Evaluation Contractor
Mercor · Surge AI · Outlier · Handshake AI · Alignerr

**$17-30/hr generalist, $30-55/hr on RLHF tiers. First cash in 1-4 weeks.
Zero acquisition cost.**

It is primary for one reason that outweighs everything else: **it is the
only play where Steven's first dollar does not depend on persuading anyone.**
No portfolio, no reviews, no Connects, no sales call. He applies, takes a
~1-hour assessment (paid at the work rate on Outlier), and gets an hourly
rate. It pays 2-3x his current trajectory for work that fits his A-grade
skills better than anything he has been selling.

**Its honest limitation:** it is a job, not a business, and hours are
volatile even when the rate is guaranteed. That is exactly why it is
primary and not sole — it buys runway, calmly, so Play 2 can be built
without panic-pricing.

### SECONDARY MONEY PLAY — Agency Back-End Production Partner
White-label documentation, QA, and research for small automation, marketing,
and ops agencies. **$20-40/hr. First cash 2-6 weeks.**

This is the **highest-ceiling** play and the only one that becomes a
business. It solves Steven's actual bottleneck — customer acquisition — by
renting someone else's solution to it. Acquisition cost is paid **once per
agency**, then amortizes to near zero, which is the structural inverse of
Upwork.

**Lead with documentation** (agencies feel that pain most), **start with QA**
(lowest liability — Steven finds problems, he can't cause them), **charge a
$150-300 paid trial** rather than working free.

### BACKUP MONEY PLAY — Productized Services via Warm Network & Local
Sheet Rescue $95 · SOP $250 · Intake Form Setup $225 · Cleanup $150 ·
Meeting→Action Plan $75.

Delivery economics are genuinely excellent ($40-90/hr). The channel is the
problem, so route these through **warm network first** (highest conversion,
zero cost, the only realistic path to cash *this week*) and **one bounded
local CSRA experiment** second — whose real payoff is a named, referenceable
case study, not the fee.

**Upwork sits inside this backup**, narrowed hard: hourly only, or fixed
with a countable ≤4-hour deliverable, ≤2 proposals per week, only on a 6/6
match, and **no new Connects purchased until a paid channel exists.**

### CAREER-BUILDING PLAY — Contract SOP Writer / Implementation Support
**$28.85-$47.12/hr** for SOP writing — the highest confirmed rate in this
report, for work Steven can do with his **unaided** human skills. Ops
assistant $19.82/hr avg. Implementation support ~$20-30/hr.

Applications are **free**. Five per weekday, 45 minutes, permanently in the
background. This is the play that ends the freelance-scramble entirely if it
lands, and it builds directly toward business analysis and automation
implementation. Steven is badly under-applying here.

### LONG-TERM ASSET PLAY — Content / YouTube
**Capped at 0-2 hours per week, or paused entirely without guilt.**
6-18 months to monetization. It is not an answer to "what makes money now,"
and treating it as one has a real cost measured in hours not spent on the
four plays above.

## THE THREE THINGS THAT MUST CHANGE

1. **Stop selling fixed-price research to strangers.** $225 ÷ 17 real hours =
   **$11.90/hr** — below the kill floor before counting losing proposals.
   The scope has no natural end and the buyer's marginal request is free.

2. **Stop treating Upwork as the business.** 27 Connects is four proposals.
   Every alternative channel in `15` costs **$0** and there are seven of them.

3. **Sell the A-grade skill, not the B-grade one.** Steven's instinct points
   at automation because it feels like the future. His money is in
   **reading, judging, verifying, and explaining** — which is both what he's
   genuinely good at *and*, in 2026, what the AI industry is buying at scale.
   The automation skills grow on hourly contracts where the clock protects
   him. They are not the offer.

## WHAT THIS PLAN DELIBERATELY DOES NOT DO

- **Does not defend Stevo AI Business, Product-01, the source-checked
  research pipeline, YouTube, or the automation-services positioning.**
  Every one was evaluated on demand and economics; none survived as a
  primary play.
- **Does not require Steven to become a salesperson before he earns.**
  Play 1 pays while Play 2's sales skill is still being built.
- **Does not require any spending.** Not one dollar. Not one Connect.
- **Does not require any new technical skill** to start earning.
- **Does not pretend the technical skills are further along than they are.**

> It was that he was selling the wrong skill, at the wrong price, through the
> only channel where he had to pay for the privilege of being ignored.

Fix the channel first. The skill is already there.

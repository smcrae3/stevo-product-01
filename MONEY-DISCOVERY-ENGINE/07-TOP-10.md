## WHAT THE SCORES ACTUALLY SAY

Two models tie at 81 for opposite reasons, and that tension is the whole
strategy:

- **M20 wins on certainty.** Perfect scores on delivery safety and
  first-customer probability. Nobody has to be persuaded. Steven applies,
  passes an assessment, and gets paid an hourly rate. It scores lowest of
  the top three on AI leverage — because in this model *Steven is the
  product*, which is precisely why it is safe.
- **M34 wins on trajectory.** Same score, better ceiling, better career
  value, better recurrence — but it requires Steven to do the thing he has
  never yet done: **get a stranger to say yes.**

The correct move is not to choose. It is to run **M20 for cash floor** while
**M34/M32 builds the durable channel** — and to keep the free-to-apply
contract lane (M37/M38) running permanently underneath both.

Notice what is *absent* from the top 10: every "build an automation for a
client" model. That is not an accident. It is where Steven's enthusiasm
points and where his grade-B skills and liability exposure both live.

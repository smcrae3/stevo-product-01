## GROUP 1 — RESEARCH & INFORMATION PRODUCTION

**M01 — Verified B2B company/prospect list building**
Buyer: small B2B sales teams, agencies, founders. Deliverable: 100-500 rows,
verified company + contact + firmographics + source column. Now: Apollo/ZoomInfo
exports full of dead data, or a cheap VA. Why pay: verification, not collection —
bounced email lists cost them deliverability. Fit: MODERATE. AI leverage: HIGH
(drafting/enrichment). QA: HIGH — every row spot-checked. Price: $150-$400 per
250 rows. Time: 3-6 hrs with AI. Risk: **volume trap** — a 500-row "verified"
list can require screening 1,500 candidates. Price per *verified* row, never per
row attempted.

**M02 — Fixed-price public-source civic/utility research reports**
*(Steven's current Upwork shape: $50 water utility, $225 firefighter.)*
Buyer: consultants, nonprofits, small research shops. Deliverable: sourced
research memo. Now: they do it themselves or hire cheap. Why pay: time.
Fit: STRONG on skill. AI leverage: HIGH. QA: HIGH. Price: $50-$225 fixed.
Time: **8-20 hrs realistically, unbounded**. Risk: **fatal** — scope has no
natural floor, "one more source" is free to the buyer and expensive to Steven.
$225 ÷ 15 hrs = **$15/hr before revisions, ~$9/hr after**. → **RED**

**M03 — Competitor / market map research deck**
Buyer: SMB marketing leads, agency strategists. Deliverable: 8-15 competitor
grid + positioning summary + sourced appendix. Now: junior does it badly, or
skipped. Why pay: decision input. Fit: MODERATE. AI: HIGH. QA: HIGH. Price:
$250-$600. Time: 4-8 hrs. Risk: buyer expects *insight*, not a table; Steven
has no analyst credibility to fall back on. → **AMBER, subcontract only**

**M04 — Vendor / software shortlist ("buyer's guide") research**
Buyer: SMB owner choosing a tool. Deliverable: 5-8 options, criteria matrix,
pricing, pros/cons, recommendation, sources. Now: 4 hours of tab-thrashing.
Why pay: decision fatigue. Fit: **STRONG** — this is literally "research +
explain + organize." AI: HIGH. QA: MEDIUM. Price: $150-$350. Time: 2-4 hrs.
Risk: buyer follows the recommendation and dislikes the tool. Mitigate: present
criteria and tradeoffs, never a single "you must buy X". → **SURVIVES**

**M05 — Online directory / association member list extraction**
Buyer: anyone building a list. Deliverable: scraped + cleaned directory.
Now: $30 on Fiverr. Why pay: barely do. Fit: STRONG but irrelevant. AI: HIGH.
Price: $30-$100. Risk: pure commodity, offshore floor, race to bottom,
possible ToS issues. → **RED**

**M06 — Niche regulated research (medical / legal / financial)**
Fit: **NO.** Credential + liability. → **RED**

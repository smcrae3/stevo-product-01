# 06 — ECONOMIC MODEL

**Modeling rules applied** (the ecommerce-research lesson):
count failed work, QA, sourcing, client communication, revision risk, and
unpaid screening. Hours below are **all-in delivery hours**, not "focused
work" hours. Where a job requires N qualified outputs, assume screening
2-4x N candidates.

Platform fee: Upwork **0-15%**, modeled at **10%**. AI-eval platforms and
direct/agency work: **0%**.

Legend — TTFC = time to first cash. CAC = customer acquisition cost, in
hours and dollars.

## THE ACQUISITION MATH THAT DECIDES EVERYTHING

Take M07, Sheets repair at $150 — the best-looking productized model:

- Delivery: 2 hrs → $150 → **$75/hr gross.** Beautiful.
- Reality on Upwork at zero reviews: ~10 proposals to win one.
- 10 proposals × 6 Connects = **60 Connects = $9.00** (Steven has **27**).
- 10 proposals × 20 min written properly = **3.3 hrs unpaid.**
- All-in: $150 − $15 fee − $9 Connects = $126 ÷ 5.3 hrs = **$23.8/hr.**

Still above the $12 floor — but it **collapses to sub-$12/hr the moment the
win rate is 1-in-20 instead of 1-in-10**, which is the realistic rate for a
brand-new profile. And Steven's current 27 Connects buys **four** attempts,
not ten.

Now the same $150 of work delivered **for an agency that already sent it to
him**: acquisition ≈ 0 → **$75/hr, sustained.**

> **The productized services are not bad. The channel is bad.**
> Identical work, identical hours, 3x the effective rate, purely as a
> function of who brings the buyer.

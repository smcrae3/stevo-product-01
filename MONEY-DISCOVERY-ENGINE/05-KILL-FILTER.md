## THE PATTERN IN THE KILLS

Almost every kill failed for one of three reasons, and they rhyme:

1. **Fixed price on unbounded scope** (M02, M10, M27-if-fixed). The buyer's
   marginal request is free; Steven's marginal hour is not.
2. **Commodity floor** (M05, M12, M22, M24, M19). Where the work is
   specifiable, it is already priced at the global minimum.
3. **Liability beyond skill** (M06, M13, M28, M30, M31, M09). Steven would be
   holding a risk he cannot price, insure, or fix at 2am.

Every survivor either **caps Steven's downside** (hourly, or bounded named
scope), **removes acquisition** (M20 platforms, M32-M34 agencies, M36-M38
employers), or **sells the one thing that is genuinely his** (M14, M20, M21,
M25 — judgment and explanation, not code).

# 08 — TOP 3 MONEY PLAYS

Three plays, deliberately from three different lanes so they fail
independently. If one dies, the other two are unaffected.

| | Play 1 | Play 2 | Play 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | AI Evaluation Contractor | Agency Back-End Production Partner | Contract Ops / SOP Writer |
| Lane | F | E | B / I |
| Role | Cash floor **now** | Durable channel | Career + stability |
| TTFC | 1-4 weeks | 2-6 weeks | 3-8 weeks |
| Net $/hr | $17-30 → $30-55 | $20-40 | $19-47 |
| Acquisition | **None** | Outreach (free) | Applications (free) |
| Weekly hours | 10-20 | 5-15 | 0 (applying) → 10-20 |

**None of the three costs a single Connect.** That is not a coincidence —
it is the design constraint.

---

## PLAY 1 — AI EVALUATION CONTRACTOR

**WHY THIS.** It is the only play where Steven's first dollar does not
require persuading anyone. He applies, takes a ~1 hour assessment (paid at
work rate on Outlier), and is paid an agreed hourly rate. It pays **$17-30/hr
generalist** against his current fixed-price trajectory of **$9-12/hr**, for
work that is a *better* fit for his A-grade skills than anything he has been
selling. Screening is bounded and sometimes paid, versus Upwork's unbounded
unpaid screening that also consumes Connects he doesn't have.

**WHO PAYS.** Mercor, Surge AI, Outlier (Scale), Handshake AI, Alignerr.
Frontier AI labs via these vendors.

**WHAT WE SELL.** Steven's judgment, by the hour.

**EXACT DELIVERABLE.** Rated and ranked model responses; written critiques
explaining *why* one output is better; factuality and source checks;
instruction-following judgments; edge-case flags.

**RATE.** $17-30/hr entry; $30-55/hr on RLHF/preference tiers with a record.

**DELIVERY TIME.** Per task, minutes to an hour. Paid weekly (Outlier) or
2-4 week first cycle (Handshake).

**HOW AI HELPS.** Barely, and that is the point — Steven *is* the human
layer. AI helps only in his own prep (understanding a rubric, drafting a
clearer critique).

**WHAT STEVEN PERSONALLY DOES.** Reads carefully. Applies a rubric
consistently. Writes a defensible reason. Does not rush. Exactly his
demonstrated CSR/QA strength.

**MUST LEARN.** Rubric discipline — grading to *their* standard, not his
instinct. Writing critiques that a reviewer can audit. Consistency across
hundreds of items when bored.

**PROOF NEEDED.** None. No portfolio. No reviews. This is the entire
advantage.

**CHANNEL.** Direct application to each platform.

**FASTEST PATH TO CUSTOMER #1.** Apply to **3+ platforms in one sitting
today.** Complete assessments within 48 hours. Do not wait for the first to
respond before applying to the next — throughput volatility is the #1 risk
and multi-platform onboarding is the only mitigation.

**RISK, STATED PLAINLY.** Rate is contractual; **hours are not.** A project
can end without notice. This is a cash floor, not a business. It buys Steven
runway to build Play 2 — that is its job.

## PLAY 2 — AGENCY BACK-END PRODUCTION PARTNER

**WHY THIS.** Steven's single worst weakness is customer acquisition. This
play deletes it. The agency already has clients, already has more delivery
work than capacity, and is structurally weakest at exactly what Steven is
strongest at: **documentation, research, and QA.** One agency relationship
produces repeat work with near-zero recurring acquisition cost — the exact
inverse of Upwork, where acquisition cost recurs on every proposal.

It also has the highest ceiling of the three: research → QA → docs →
build-assist is a real ladder inside one account.

**WHO PAYS.** Owners of 2-15 person automation agencies, marketing agencies,
small consultancies, VA agencies, research shops. Findable in the n8n
community jobs board, Make.com partner directories, agency Slack/Discord
communities, LinkedIn, and r/agency-type forums.

**WHAT WE SELL.** Bounded back-end production capacity, white-labeled.

**EXACT DELIVERABLES (the ladder, in order of how easy they are to say yes to):**
1. **Client handover documentation & SOPs** for builds they've shipped —
   $25-40/hr. *Lead with this.* Agencies know their docs are bad and it
   costs them retention.
2. **Pre-delivery QA passes** on their builds — $20-35/hr. Steven finds
   problems; he doesn't cause them. Lowest-liability entry point.
3. **Research packs** on their template and brand — $20-35/hr or
   $150-400/deliverable.
4. **Build-assist** (Make.com modules, Sheets work) under their review —
   only after trust exists.

**RATE.** $20-40/hr, or fixed per bounded deliverable. **First job: offer a
small paid trial, not free work.** Free work signals desperation and
attracts the worst agencies.

**DELIVERY TIME.** 2-8 hours per bounded unit; 48-72 hour turnaround.

**HOW AI HELPS.** Heavily — first-draft docs, research synthesis, structure.
Steven's edit and verification pass is what makes it deliverable.

**WHAT STEVEN PERSONALLY DOES.** Outreach. Scoping calls (his A-grade
skill). Interviewing the agency's builder. Verification. Final QA. Never
shipping AI output unread.

**MUST LEARN.** Agency-side vocabulary and expectations. Turnaround
discipline. Writing to *someone else's* brand voice.

**PROOF NEEDED.** Two artifacts, built this week from his own past work:
one **sample SOP** and one **sample QA bug sheet**. Real, specific, redacted
if needed. That is the entire portfolio requirement.

**CHANNEL.** Direct outreach — 10 well-researched messages beat 100 generic
ones. Communities first, LinkedIn second.

**FASTEST PATH TO CUSTOMER #1.** Build the two samples (≈3 hrs), then send
10 personalized messages to agencies whose *actual work you looked at*, each
naming a specific gap you can fill. Offer a $150-300 paid trial deliverable.

**RISK.** Rate compression if Steven becomes dependent on one agency, and
slow, silent rejection. Mitigate: never fewer than three live conversations.

## PLAY 3 — CONTRACT OPERATIONS / SOP WRITER

**WHY THIS.** It is **free to pursue**, it pays the highest confirmed rates
in this document (**SOP writer $28.85-$47.12/hr**, ops assistant
$15.38-$22.36/hr), and it is the only play that directly builds the career
Steven says he wants — business analysis, implementation, operations. It
runs in the background of the other two and costs nothing but application
time.

**WHO PAYS.** SMBs, startups, agencies, healthcare and logistics ops teams
hiring part-time/contract remote staff via Indeed, ZipRecruiter, LinkedIn,
Wellfound, and industry boards.

**WHAT WE SELL.** Steven's time as a contractor.

**EXACT DELIVERABLE.** Depends on the role: written SOPs and process docs,
operational admin, data/reporting support, onboarding coordination.

**RATE.** $28-47/hr SOP writing; $19-22/hr ops assistant; ~$20-30/hr
implementation support.

**HOW AI HELPS.** Tailoring applications fast (10 quality applications in
the time 3 used to take), and drafting SOPs once employed.

**WHAT STEVEN PERSONALLY DOES.** Applies consistently. Interviews well —
his strongest single asset in a hiring context.

**MUST LEARN.** Nothing new to *start*. To convert: how to talk about
process work in employer language.

**PROOF NEEDED.** The same 2 sample SOPs as Play 2. **One artifact,
two plays** — this is why it is Day 1's task.

**CHANNEL.** Direct application. Zero cost.

**FASTEST PATH TO CUSTOMER #1.** 5 applications/day, every weekday, using
the SOP samples as portfolio. Expect a 3-8 week cycle and do not treat
silence as failure.

**RISK.** Slow, and conversion is low. It must never be the *only* play —
which is why it is third, not first.

---

## HOW THE THREE INTERLOCK

- **The same 2 sample SOPs** serve Play 2 and Play 3. Build once, use twice.
- **Play 1 funds the time** to do Plays 2 and 3 without panic-bidding.
- **Play 2 is the only one that becomes a business.** Plays 1 and 3 are jobs.
- **Play 1 is the hedge, Play 3 is the floor, Play 2 is the upside.**
- If Play 1 throughput dries up → Plays 2/3 unaffected.
  If agency outreach fails → Play 1 still pays.

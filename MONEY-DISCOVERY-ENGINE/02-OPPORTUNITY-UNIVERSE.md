# 02 — OPPORTUNITY UNIVERSE (LANE SCAN)

Scanned before narrowing. Verdicts here are lane-level; individual models
are graded in `04` and killed in `05`.

## LANE A — FREELANCE MARKETPLACES
Upwork, Fiverr, Contra, PeoplePerHour, Freelancer, Guru.

**Verdict: DEMOTED to backup, not eliminated.**

Real demand exists, but Steven enters with the three worst possible
starting conditions simultaneously: no reviews, no portfolio, and ~27
Connects (≈4 standard proposals at 6 Connects each). The economics at the
entry tier are brutal — the median fixed-price data-entry budget on Upwork
is **$33**, and market-research profiles list from **$9/hr**. Fixed-price
research at $50-$225 is where Steven currently is, and it is the worst cell
in the worst lane.

Keep Upwork only for: **hourly contracts**, **Google Sheets repair**, and
**paid trials**. Fiverr is viable but is a 4-12 week ranking game, not cash
this week.

## LANE B — CONTRACT / REMOTE PART-TIME WORK
**Verdict: STRONG. Underrated by Steven. Applications are FREE.**

Remote operations assistant averages **$19.82/hr** (typical band
$15.38-$22.36). Remote SOP/technical writer averages **$38.94/hr** (band
$28.85-$47.12). Tens of thousands of listings. No Connects, no portfolio
gate, no client acquisition — just applications. This lane is the single
biggest thing Steven is under-using, and it doubles as career construction.

## LANE C — AI-ASSISTED BUSINESS PRODUCTION
**Verdict: REAL, but it is a *delivery method*, not a lane.**

Research packs, SOPs, competitive scans, transcript transformation, data
cleanup. Steven + AI genuinely compresses 5-8 hours into 1-3. That is a
margin advantage — but only *after* a buyer exists. This lane has no
acquisition mechanism of its own, which is precisely Steven's bottleneck.
Route it through Lane E (agencies) or Lane B (contracts), not cold.

## LANE D — LOCAL BUSINESS SERVICES (CSRA / Augusta)
**Verdict: MEDIUM. Real, slow, high-friction.**

Small businesses do have messy spreadsheets and no intake process. But the
sales cycle is walk-in/referral, conversion is low, budgets are $200-$800,
and Steven has no local proof. Worth **one bounded experiment**, not a
strategy. Its true value is that a single local client produces a *named,
real, referenceable case study* — which unlocks everything else.

## LANE E — SUBCONTRACTING / WHITE-LABEL
**Verdict: STRONGEST STRATEGIC FIT. This is the acquisition fix.**

Automation agencies, marketing agencies, VA agencies, small consultancies
and research firms all have customers already and are structurally
short on back-end production capacity. Active 2026 hiring threads exist in
the n8n community and adjacent spaces for freelance/agency automation help
and white-label support. Steven performs bounded work; **the agency owns the
client**. This eliminates his single worst weakness. Slower to first dollar
than Lane F, but far more durable.

## LANE F — AI TRAINING / EVALUATION WORK
**Verdict: STRONGEST IMMEDIATE FIT. Highest first-dollar probability.**

Mercor, Surge AI, Outlier, Handshake AI, Alignerr, Scale. National average
AI-trainer pay ~**$31/hr**. Generalist tiers: Handshake ~**$17/hr**, Outlier
**$15-30/hr**, RLHF/preference annotation **$30-55/hr** depending on
platform. Onboarding is a **50-60 minute assessment** (Handshake) or a
**1-5 hour** ramp (Outlier, where the qualification assessment is paid at
the work rate). First payout typically **2-4 weeks**; Outlier pays weekly.

Zero customer acquisition. Zero portfolio requirement. Pays for exactly
Steven's A-grade cluster: read, judge, explain, verify. The 2024-era advice
("this is scam-adjacent gig work") is out of date — the 2026 market is
larger, better-paid, and structurally hungry for careful generalists.

**Caveat that must not be glossed:** throughput is volatile. A $90/hr
project offering 6 hrs/week pays less than a $30/hr project offering 25.
Steven should onboard to **multiple** platforms, not one.

## LANE G — DIGITAL PRODUCTS
**Verdict: KILLED for now.** No buyer evidence, no audience, no proof, and
it converts scarce build hours into an asset with an unknown demand curve.
Revisit only after a service produces repeat customers who *ask* for a
template.

## LANE H — CONTENT / INBOUND (YouTube)
**Verdict: TIER 4 ASSET, deprioritized to near-zero.** Correctly excluded
from the money question. 6-18 months to monetization. Steven needs cash. Cap
at 0-2 hrs/week or pause entirely without guilt.

## LANE I — CAREER-ALIGNED PAID WORK
**Verdict: STRONG, and it overlaps Lane B.**

Implementation support, operations analyst support, onboarding coordinator,
business-systems assistant, RevOps assistant. These build directly toward
business analysis / automation implementation / Power Platform — Steven's
stated destination. Free to apply. Should run permanently in the background.

## LANE J — OTHER
Considered and rejected as material: reselling/arbitrage (capital + risk),
transcription (AI-collapsed pricing), survey/microtask sites (sub-$12/hr),
bookkeeping (credential + liability), grant writing (credential), and
"AI agency" reseller programs (these sell *to* Steven, not through him —
he is the customer in that model, which is the opposite of the mission).

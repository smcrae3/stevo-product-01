# PART A — PHASE 14: HOURLY VS FIXED-PRICE STRATEGY

## THE LESSON, STATED NUMERICALLY

Steven's $225 firefighter research proposal, modeled honestly (`06`):
scoping 1h + research 8-12h + verification 2h + writing 2-3h + revisions
1-2h = **14-20 hours**. After Upwork's ~10% fee: **$11.90/hr.** Below the
kill floor — before counting losing proposals or Connects.

The mechanism is simple and it is structural, not a mistake Steven made:

> On a fixed-price contract with undefined scope, **the buyer's marginal
> request costs them nothing and costs Steven everything.** "Could you also
> check these five sources?" is free to ask and is two unpaid hours.

Fixed price is a bet that Steven can predict the work. He is new. He cannot
predict the work. **Therefore: bias hard to hourly.**

## THE RULE

> **DEFAULT TO HOURLY. Fixed price is the exception and must be earned.**

## WHEN FIXED PRICE IS ACCEPTABLE — all five must be true
1. The deliverable is **countable** ("100 verified rows", "one 3-page SOP",
   "one named formula fixed") — not "research X" or "help with Y".
2. Steven has **done this exact thing before** and knows his real hours.
3. Estimated hours ≤ **4**, so a 2x overrun is survivable.
4. **Revisions are capped in writing** — one round, then hourly.
5. Implied rate at his **worst realistic** hour estimate is still **≥ $30/hr**.

If any one fails → hourly, or decline.

## WHEN FIXED PRICE IS FORBIDDEN
- Any research with an open source list.
- Any "clean up my data" without seeing the data first.
- Any Apps Script or automation work. **Always.** Debugging is unbounded and
  Steven is grade B — he cannot cap his own downside.
- Any job where the buyer hasn't clearly defined "done".
- Any first engagement with a buyer who has no payment history.

## THE PREFERRED STRUCTURE: PAID TRIAL → HOURLY
For any uncertain work:
> "Rather than guess at a fixed price, let's start with a small paid piece —
> [2 hours / $150] on [the smallest real slice]. You see the quality on
> something real, I see the actual data, and then we scope the rest
> accurately. If it's not right, you've risked two hours."

This does three things at once: de-risks the buyer (raising win rate),
protects Steven from unbounded scope, and converts an unknown into a
measurement. **Use this language everywhere — Upwork, agencies, local.**

## THE MILESTONE RULE
If a client insists on fixed price for a larger project: break it into
milestones ≤ $300 each, each independently scoped and funded in escrow, with
the right to re-scope between milestones. Never one large fixed milestone.

# PART B — PHASE 15: SEARCH STRATEGY

## WHY "AI AUTOMATION" IS THE WRONG SEARCH
It returns jobs demanding senior engineering (grade D), attracts 50+
proposals from specialists, and pits Steven against people who can debug
unaided. It is the search that *feels* like his business and is the worst
one for his skills.

## SEARCH FAMILIES — ranked

### FAMILY 1 — SHEETS RESCUE (best fit, highest urgency)
`google sheets fix` · `google sheets formula help` · `spreadsheet broken` ·
`google sheets cleanup` · `excel to google sheets` · `spreadsheet organize`
Filters: **Hourly OR fixed ≤$300** · posted <24h · <10 proposals ·
payment verified
Why: small, urgent, bounded, inspectable, buyer is blocked today.

### FAMILY 2 — DOCUMENTATION & PROCESS (best economics)
`SOP writer` · `process documentation` · `write standard operating
procedures` · `document our process` · `create training manual` ·
`workflow documentation`
Filters: **Hourly preferred** · $25+/hr · <15 proposals
Why: $28-47/hr confirmed market, pure A-grade skill, low liability.

### FAMILY 3 — OPERATIONS SUPPORT (recurring, relationship-building)
`operations assistant` · `virtual assistant spreadsheets` ·
`admin support google workspace` · `business operations support` ·
`ops assistant part time`
Filters: **Hourly only** · ongoing/long-term · $20+/hr
Why: recurring hours beat one-off projects. A 10 hr/week ongoing contract
at $22/hr is worth more than five $150 projects and costs Connects once.

### FAMILY 4 — RESEARCH, NARROWLY (hourly only)
`research assistant hourly` · `market research hourly` ·
`competitor research` · `company research list`
Filters: **HOURLY ONLY — no exceptions** · $20+/hr · <20 proposals
Why: research is where Steven is strong and where fixed price destroyed him.
The filter, not the skill, is what changed.

### FAMILY 5 — QA & VERIFICATION (lowest competition)
`data QA` · `quality check spreadsheet` · `verify data accuracy` ·
`fact check` · `proofread report` · `AI content review`
Filters: hourly or ≤$300 fixed
Why: thinnest competition of any family; perfect skill match.

### FAMILY 6 — APPS SCRIPT, TINY ONLY (hourly, capped)
`google apps script small fix` · `apps script bug` · `google forms automation`
Filters: **HOURLY ONLY** · ≤10 hr estimate · buyer has hired before
Why: skill-building on someone else's clock, with the clock protecting him.
**Never fixed price here.** Confirmed active demand exists in these
categories; rate distribution unverified (AG-3).

## HARD EXCLUSION FILTERS — do not bid, regardless of appeal
| Filter | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Proposals already submitted | **>20** (>15 preferred) |
| Payment method | **Unverified → skip** |
| Client hire rate | <50% with 5+ posts → skip |
| Fixed price ÷ realistic hours | **<$30/hr → skip** |
| Deliverable size vs budget | "100-page report for $100" → skip |
| Skills required | Anything grade D → skip |
| Job age | >72 hours → skip (already shortlisted) |
| Client spend | $0 spent + $0 hires + vague post → skip |
| Test requirement | Unpaid test >30 min → skip |
| Boost required to compete | Skip — he can't afford it |

## THE CONNECT ALLOCATION RULE (~27 Connects)
That is **four** standard proposals. Treat each as $50 of Steven's time,
because that is what writing a good one costs.

**Spend a Connect only if ALL are true:**
- [ ] Fits Family 1, 2, 3, or 5 (not 4 or 6 unless exceptional)
- [ ] Hourly, or fixed with a countable ≤4-hour deliverable
- [ ] <15 proposals
- [ ] Payment verified
- [ ] Steven can name, in one sentence, why he specifically fits
- [ ] He could deliver it *today* if awarded

**Six boxes or don't bid.** Zero proposals this week is a perfectly good
outcome if nothing passed.

**Do not buy more Connects until a paid channel exists.** Buying Connects to
fix a conversion problem is buying more lottery tickets because the last ones
lost.

## PROPOSAL TEMPLATE (short, specific, no preamble)
> [First line: name their exact problem in their words.]
>
> I'd approach it like this: [2-3 concrete sentences — the actual method,
> not "I have 5 years experience".]
>
> [One sentence of relevant, honest, specific proof.]
>
> I'd suggest starting hourly, or with a small paid first piece so you can
> see the quality before committing. My estimate is [X hours] — I'll tell you
> immediately if it looks like more.
>
> One question: [a real question that shows he read the post and understood
> the actual problem.]
>
> — Steven

**Never:** "I am excited about this opportunity", "I have extensive
experience in...", or any paragraph that could be pasted into another job
post unchanged.

## WEEKLY CADENCE (until income exists)
- **15 minutes/day** scanning Families 1, 2, 3, 5.
- **0-2 proposals/week**, only on 6/6 matches.
- Everything else goes to Play 1 and Play 2.

Upwork gets 5% of Steven's time until it earns more.

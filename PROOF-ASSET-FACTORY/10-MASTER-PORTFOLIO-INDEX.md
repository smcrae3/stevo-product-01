### 1. SOP / Process Documentation
**Files:** `01-SOP-DEMONSTRATION.md` · `02-SOP-PROCESS-MAP.md` ·
`03-SOP-CASE-STUDY.md`
**What it shows:** A complete, usable standard operating procedure for a
customer-inquiry intake process — document control, roles, step-by-step
procedure, decision logic, exception handling, escalation rules, and a
companion process map showing happy/exception/escalation paths as one
connected flow.
**Ideal buyer:** Agencies needing client handover documentation; small
businesses without written procedures; hiring managers for
documentation/SOP-writer roles.
**Skills demonstrated:** Process interviewing logic, structured technical
writing, decision-table design, exception handling, plain-English
explanation of operational logic.
**AI role:** Initial structural drafting from interview-style notes;
language tightening.
**Human role:** Interview question design (the two questions that surface
exceptions), internal-consistency verification of every decision point,
terminology consistency check, final formatting.
**Status:** **READY**

### 2. QA / Workflow Testing
**Files:** `04-QA-TEST-PACK.md` · `05-QA-BUG-REPORTS.md` ·
`06-QA-CASE-STUDY.md`
**What it shows:** A 38-case test pack and 8 detailed bug reports against a
fictional AI-assisted automation, covering happy path, invalid input,
duplicate detection, failure simulation, structural fragility, and recovery/
replay behavior — with severity ranking and pattern-level observations.
**Ideal buyer:** Automation and no-code agencies needing pre-delivery QA;
QA analyst or automation-adjacent contract roles.
**Skills demonstrated:** Adversarial test-case design, severity
classification, reproducible bug reporting, pattern recognition across
findings (e.g., "silent failure" as a recurring root cause).
**AI role:** Generated an initial category checklist quickly; conceptual
execution support.
**Human role:** Design of structural/fragility test cases beyond an
obvious checklist, execution and result recording, root-cause pattern
analysis across findings, severity judgment calls.
**Status:** **READY**

### 3. Requirements / Implementation Brief
**File:** `07-REQUIREMENTS-BRIEF.md`
**What it shows:** A full requirements document for a "combine quarterly
spreadsheets into one dataset" project — functional and nonfunctional
requirements, header-normalization strategy, duplicate logic, audit
logging, open questions, out-of-scope boundaries, and a test plan.
**Ideal buyer:** No-code/automation agencies (as a requirements-gathering
sample); implementation coordinator or business analyst-track roles.
**Skills demonstrated:** Requirements elicitation, distinguishing
assumptions from confirmed facts, scoping discipline (explicit out-of-scope
section), designing for a specific realistic failure mode (header drift)
rather than only the happy path.
**AI role:** Structural drafting of the requirements categories.
**Human role:** Identifying the open questions that materially change the
design (e.g., whether Order ID is truly unique), the header-normalization
approach, and the acceptance criteria.
**Status:** **READY**

### 4. Customer Experience Operations Analysis
**File:** `08-CX-OPERATIONS-ANALYSIS.md`
**What it shows:** Analysis of 20 fictional customer-service interactions —
category taxonomy, root-cause summary, retention risk signals, and 5 ranked
operational improvements.
**Ideal buyer:** CX/CS consultancies; customer experience operations
contract roles; any agency serving support-heavy clients.
**Skills demonstrated:** Building a taxonomy that tolerates overlap rather
than forcing false precision, separating stated reason from underlying
signal, ranking recommendations by frequency × ease of fix rather than
listing them undifferentiated.
**AI role:** Organizing the summary table; drafting initial category
groupings.
**Human role:** All judgment calls — the taxonomy design itself, the
root-cause reads, and the ranked recommendations, drawing on real
professional customer-service experience (disclosed as informing the
realism of the fictional dataset, not as real company data).
**Status:** **READY**

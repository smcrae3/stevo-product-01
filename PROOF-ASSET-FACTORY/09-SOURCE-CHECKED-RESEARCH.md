## THE METHOD: AI DISCOVERS → SOURCE PROVES → HUMAN APPROVES

This is the three-step discipline every claim in a source-checked research
deliverable goes through before it ships:

**AI DISCOVERS.** AI is used to generate candidate claims quickly — "this
organization likely offers X," "this company's pricing is probably in Y
range." This step is fast and useful and **not trustworthy on its own.**

**SOURCE PROVES.** Every candidate claim is checked against a **primary
source** — the organization's own published page, a filing, a direct
statement — not a secondary summary of a summary. The source is opened,
read, and the specific supporting text or figure is noted, along with the
date it was checked (facts like pricing and staff counts change).

**HUMAN APPROVES.** A human decides, for each claim, whether the source
actually supports it as stated, assigns a confidence level, and — critically
— **leaves the field blank rather than guessing** when no adequate source
exists. A blank field is not a failure of the research; a wrong field
presented as fact is.

This is the exact discipline that separates a research deliverable someone
can act on from an AI-generated list that looks complete but isn't checkable.

## THE SCHEMA

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Claim** | The specific, falsifiable statement being made |
| **Source** | The specific document/page the claim is drawn from |
| **Source type** | Primary (company's own site/filing) / Secondary (news, review site) / Tertiary (aggregator, unverified list) |
| **Date checked** | When the source was actually opened and read — not when the research started |
| **Confidence** | High (primary source, current, unambiguous) / Medium (secondary source, or primary but ambiguous) / Low (best available, should be independently re-verified before being relied upon) |
| **Notes** | Anything a reader needs — a caveat, a reason the source might be stale, a discrepancy between two sources |

**The rule that makes this trustworthy:** a claim with no source that meets
at least "Medium" confidence is **left blank**, not filled with a
plausible-sounding guess. An empty cell is honest. A filled cell with a
fabricated or unchecked source is not research — it's the exact failure
mode this method exists to prevent.

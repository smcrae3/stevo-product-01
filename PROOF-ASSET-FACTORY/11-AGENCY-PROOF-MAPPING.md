# 11 — AGENCY PROOF MAPPING

Which proof asset to send first, by agency type. Matches the agency
categories from the prior mission's `03-AGENCY-SUBCONTRACT-MODEL.md`.

---

## AUTOMATION AGENCY (Make.com, Zapier, n8n builders)

**Best proof to send first:** QA / Workflow Testing pack
(`04-QA-TEST-PACK.md` + `05-QA-BUG-REPORTS.md`)
**Why:** This is the exact bounded, low-liability service (Offer 2 from the
prior mission) that's easiest for an automation agency to say yes to — they
recognize the workflow shape immediately and can judge the quality of the
test cases against their own experience.
**Secondary proof:** SOP / Process Documentation (handoff-packet framing)
**What NOT to send:** The Requirements Brief — automation agencies typically
already own requirements gathering internally; leading with it can read as
overreach into their process rather than filling a gap.

## RESEARCH AGENCY / RESEARCH-ADJACENT FIRMS

**Best proof to send first:** Source-Checked Research
(`09-SOURCE-CHECKED-RESEARCH.md`, **only once live-verified** — never send
the placeholder version)
**Why:** Directly demonstrates the exact discipline they're buying —
verification rigor, not just information-gathering speed.
**Secondary proof:** CX Operations Analysis (shows structured analytical
writing beyond pure lookup work)
**What NOT to send:** The QA test pack — unrelated to their buying need and
dilutes the pitch.

## CX / CUSTOMER-EXPERIENCE CONSULTANCY

**Best proof to send first:** CX Operations Analysis
(`08-CX-OPERATIONS-ANALYSIS.md`)
**Why:** This is Steven's most defensible piece — professional customer-
service background gives it real credibility, and it's the only asset
where his direct experience (not AI assistance) is the substance.
**Secondary proof:** SOP / Process Documentation (shows he can also
formalize the fixes his analysis recommends)
**What NOT to send:** Requirements Brief — too far from their domain to be
relevant as a first impression.

## NO-CODE AGENCY (Bubble, Webflow, Airtable builders)

**Best proof to send first:** Requirements / Implementation Brief
(`07-REQUIREMENTS-BRIEF.md`)
**Why:** No-code builds live and die on clear requirements up front — this
asset shows Steven can gather and structure them, including the discipline
of flagging open questions rather than guessing.
**Secondary proof:** QA / Workflow Testing pack
**What NOT to send:** CX Operations Analysis — unrelated to their build-
focused buying need.

## VA AGENCY / OPERATIONS SUPPORT AGENCY

**Best proof to send first:** SOP / Process Documentation
(`01-SOP-DEMONSTRATION.md` + `02-SOP-PROCESS-MAP.md`)
**Why:** VA agencies place people into exactly this kind of
documentation/spreadsheet-operations work; the SOP is the clearest,
fastest-to-evaluate demonstration of "can this person structure operational
knowledge."
**Secondary proof:** Requirements Brief (shows spreadsheet/data-operations
thinking, relevant to the "combine quarterly files" problem class many VA
clients have)
**What NOT to send:** Source-Checked Research — too specialized a framing
for a generalist VA placement conversation.

---

## THE RULE ACROSS ALL FIVE

**Never send more than two assets in a first outreach.** Per
`16-AGENCY-ATTACHMENT-PACKS.md`, two well-matched samples read as
"this person knows what I need." Five samples read as "this person is
hoping something sticks" — and it makes the reviewer do the matching work
Steven should have already done for them.

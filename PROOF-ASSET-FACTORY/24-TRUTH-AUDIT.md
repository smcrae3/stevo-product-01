## CORRECTIONS REQUIRED

Three flagged items above require an actual text fix, not just a
classification note. Applying them now:

### Correction 1 — SOP Training Notes section (Asset 1)
**Original:** "the two most common new-hire errors observed in walkthroughs"
**Problem:** implies real walkthroughs with real people occurred.
**Fix:** change to "the two most common new-hire errors **this kind of
process tends to produce**" — removes the implied observation while keeping
the substantive content (the two error types themselves are a reasonable,
disclosed inference, not a claimed data point).

### Correction 2 — QA Case Study (Asset 6)
**Original:** "roughly a third of the 38 test cases... came from that kind
of targeted thinking"
**Problem:** states a specific fraction with no shown count.
**Fix:** change to "several of the 38 test cases — particularly the
structural-fragility and recovery-related ones — came from that kind of
targeted thinking" — same substantive point, without a fabricated-precision
statistic.

### Correction 3 — SOP Sales Demo (Mission 19)
**Original:** "this is the actual finished output from the 'before' above,
not a mockup"
**Problem:** "not a mockup" could be misread as "not fictional" by a fast
reader, despite the file's top-of-page disclaimer.
**Fix:** change to "this is the actual finished output produced from the
'before' above **within this demonstration** — not a separately mocked-up
example unrelated to the input shown" — keeps the true point (the
transformation is real, traceable, and not staged-looking) while removing
the ambiguous phrase.

**Action:** apply all three corrections directly to the source files rather
than only documenting them here.

## "I give you this..."

A complete, structured SOP:

- **Document control** — versioned, owned, dated
- **Roles and responsibilities** — who does what, with a defined fallback
  for anything unclear
- **A concrete urgency table** — with example signals, not vague adjectives
- **Step-by-step procedure** — numbered, with decision points spelled out
- **An exceptions section** — built specifically from the failure you
  mentioned (like the "same customer emailed three times and nobody
  noticed" story), not generic boilerplate
- **Escalation rules** — what triggers escalation, and to whom, immediately
- **A one-page quick reference and checklist** — for someone to use on the
  job without re-reading the whole document

## WHAT HAPPENS IN BETWEEN

I don't just clean up your words and hand them back. Where your notes had a
gap or a contradiction — like whether billing goes to one specific person
"unless it's quick," without ever defining what counts as quick — I ask you
one direct question instead of guessing. Most of these are answered in a
single sentence. That's part of the 48 hours, not an extra step.

---

## SCOPE OF "48 HOURS"

This turnaround applies to **one standard, bounded process** — something
you could describe in a single 20-30 minute recording, covering one
end-to-end workflow (like the intake example above). It does not apply to:

- Multiple processes at once *(each additional process is quoted
  separately)*
- A process that's still actively changing week to week
- Anything requiring regulatory or compliance-specific documentation

If your process is more complex than that, I'll tell you upfront what a
realistic timeline looks like — not stretch the 48-hour promise to cover
something it wasn't built for.

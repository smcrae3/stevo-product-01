## WHY THIS VERSION IS STRUCTURALLY STRONGER THAN THE PRIOR MISSION'S

The prior outreach templates promised samples ("two samples so you can
judge the standard"). These templates **are backed by samples that actually
exist and can be inspected in under two minutes.** The specific change: the
prior mission's long email described what the samples would show in the
abstract; this version names the exact finding type (silent-failure
testing, the resolved billing-routing contradiction) that a reviewer will
actually see if they open the link — which is only possible to write
truthfully now that the assets exist.

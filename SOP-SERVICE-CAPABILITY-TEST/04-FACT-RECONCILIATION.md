## Confirmation

- **No factual inventions:** every numbered procedure step, warning, and
  checklist item in `02-POLISHED-SOP.docx` traces back to a fact already
  present in `01-RAW-NOTES.docx`, per the table above.
- **No missing mandatory facts:** all 17 distinct facts from the raw notes
  (after collapsing the one deliberate repetition, #16) appear somewhere in
  the final SOP.
- **Nothing guessed:** the one place the raw notes admit a gap (remote/hybrid
  first week) is carried forward as an open clarification item, not
  invented or quietly dropped.

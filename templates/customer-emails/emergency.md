# Customer Email — Emergency

**When used:** `Urgency` = `Emergency`

**Subject:** We received your emergency request — [Business Name] is on it

**Body:**

```
Hi [Customer Name],

Thanks for reaching out to [Business Name]. We understand this is an
emergency, and we're treating it as a top priority.

Here's what happens next:
- A member of our team will call you at [Phone] within [X minutes/hour]
  to confirm details and get someone on the way.
- If your safety is at risk right now (gas smell, active flooding,
  electrical sparking, fire), please also call [Emergency Line / 911 if
  applicable] immediately — don't wait on this email.

What you told us:
"[Message Summary]"

We'll be in touch shortly.

[Business Name]
[Phone] | [Business Hours]
```

**Notes for setup:**
- Keep this the fastest, shortest template — customers in a genuine
  emergency don't want to read a long email.
- `[X minutes/hour]` should match your business's real emergency
  response-time commitment. Don't promise a time you can't hit.
- The safety-risk line should reflect your trade (e.g. HVAC: "no heat in
  freezing weather" isn't a call-911 situation, but a gas smell is).

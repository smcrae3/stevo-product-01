# Customer Email — Routine

**When used:** `Urgency` = `Routine`

**Subject:** Thanks for contacting [Business Name]

**Body:**

```
Hi [Customer Name],

Thanks for reaching out to [Business Name] about your [Service Type]
request. We've got your message and will follow up within
[X business days] to schedule a time that works for you.

What you told us:
"[Message Summary]"

If anything is more urgent than it sounds, just reply to this email or
call us at [Phone] and we'll bump it up.

Talk soon,
[Business Name]
[Phone] | [Business Hours]
```

**Notes for setup:**
- `[X business days]` should match your normal (non-urgent) response
  time — commonly 1-3 business days.

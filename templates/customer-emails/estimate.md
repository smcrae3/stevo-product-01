# Customer Email — Estimate

**When used:** `Urgency` = `Estimate`

**Subject:** Your estimate request — [Business Name]

**Body:**

```
Hi [Customer Name],

Thanks for thinking of [Business Name] for your [Service Type] project.
We'd be happy to put together an estimate for you.

Here's what happens next:
- We'll reach out within [X business days] to ask a few quick questions
  and, if needed, schedule an on-site visit to give you an accurate
  number.
- In the meantime, feel free to reply with any photos, measurements, or
  details that might help.

What you told us:
"[Message Summary]"

Looking forward to working with you,
[Business Name]
[Phone] | [Business Hours]
```

**Notes for setup:**
- Estimate requests are rarely time-sensitive, but should never be
  ignored — a slow estimate response is a common reason businesses lose
  future work. Keep `[X business days]` realistic but prompt.

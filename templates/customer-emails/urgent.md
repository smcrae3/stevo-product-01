# Customer Email — Urgent

**When used:** `Urgency` = `Urgent`

**Subject:** We got your request — [Business Name] will be in touch today

**Body:**

```
Hi [Customer Name],

Thanks for reaching out to [Business Name] about your [Service Type]
issue. We know this kind of thing is disruptive, and we want to get it
sorted quickly.

Here's what happens next:
- Our team will call or email you at [Phone/Email] within [X hours]
  today to schedule a visit.
- If anything changes or gets worse in the meantime, reply to this email
  or call us at [Phone].

What you told us:
"[Message Summary]"

Talk soon,
[Business Name]
[Phone] | [Business Hours]
```

**Notes for setup:**
- `[X hours]` should reflect a realistic same-day response window.
- This template assumes a same-day but not immediate response — adjust
  if your business commits to faster turnaround.

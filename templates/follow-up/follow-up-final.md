# Follow-Up Email — Final Check-In

**When used:** Manually sent by the owner/office if a first follow-up
(see [`follow-up-1.md`](follow-up-1.md)) went unanswered for another
[X days], and the lead is still not scheduled.

**Subject:** Should we close out your request? — [Business Name]

**Body:**

```
Hi [Customer Name],

We haven't heard back, so we want to check in one more time before we
close out your [Service Type] request in our system.

If you'd still like to move forward, just reply or call [Phone] any
time — happy to help. If your plans changed, no worries at all, and
thanks for thinking of [Business Name].

[Business Name]
[Phone] | [Business Hours]
```

**Notes for setup:**
- After sending this, if there's still no response, set `Status` to
  `Completed` (closed out, not serviced) and note the reason in `Notes`
  so the lead doesn't linger indefinitely as "New."
- This template is a courtesy close-out, not a sales push — it's meant
  to keep the pipeline clean, and occasionally it does win back a
  customer who simply got busy.

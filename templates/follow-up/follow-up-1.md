# Follow-Up Email — First Check-In

**When used:** Manually sent by the owner/office (not automated in V1)
when a lead's `Follow-Up Date` has passed and `Status` is still `New`,
`Acknowledged`, or `Owner Notified` (i.e. no visit scheduled yet).

**Subject:** Following up on your [Service Type] request — [Business Name]

**Body:**

```
Hi [Customer Name],

Just following up on your recent [Service Type] request — we want to
make sure this didn't fall through the cracks.

Are you still looking to get this taken care of? If so, reply to this
email or call us at [Phone] and we'll get you scheduled.

Thanks,
[Business Name]
[Phone] | [Business Hours]
```

**Notes for setup:**
- This is intentionally short and low-pressure — the goal is to confirm
  the customer is still interested, not to sell.

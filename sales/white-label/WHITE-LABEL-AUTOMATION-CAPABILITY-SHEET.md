# Stevo AI Business — White-Label Automation Capability Sheet

## Positioning

Stevo AI Business is the technical automation builder agencies plug in behind their own brand when
they have more Make.com / Sheets / webhook work than build capacity.

## Ideal agency partner

- Sells automation, systemization, or "done-for-you ops" to their own clients but is sales/account-
  management heavy and technical-build light
- Needs a Make.com, Google Sheets, webhook, or simple-integration build done fast, scoped, and
  white-label — not a full agency partnership up front
- Comfortable with NDA / no-logo, no-credit delivery
- Has (or is building) an overflow bench instead of hiring a full-time automation engineer

## What we can safely build today

- Make.com scenarios: lead routing, form → CRM/Sheets sync, notification/follow-up sequences
- Google Sheets automation: scripted workflows, data sync, templated reporting
- Webhook-based integrations between common SaaS tools (forms, CRMs, calendars, Slack/email)
- Simple, well-defined API-to-API connections
- Repair/debugging of an existing small Make.com or Sheets automation that has broken or drifted
- Testing against a written acceptance checklist, plus a recorded walkthrough and technical handoff

## What we do NOT take

- Advanced/expert-level n8n work (not yet demonstrated at that depth)
- Voice AI or telephony integrations
- Regulated healthcare or financial production systems
- Custom enterprise software
- Anything requiring a guaranteed turnaround time
- Promises of ROI or conversion increases
- Unlimited or open-ended support

## Delivery process

1. Agency sends a workflow brief (what it should do, systems involved, what "done" looks like)
2. Stevo returns a fixed written scope and price — pilot at $149 for the first job
3. Build happens against that scope only — no silent scope expansion
4. Build is tested against the written acceptance criteria
5. Short recorded walkthrough + written technical handoff delivered
6. One revision round included; anything beyond scope is quoted separately

## QA / testing process

- Every build is checked against the acceptance criteria written into the scope before handoff
- Failure paths (bad input, missing field, failed API call) are tested where relevant to the build
- Nothing is marked "done" without a pass against that written checklist

## Documentation & handoff

- Written technical handoff: what was built, how it works, where credentials/connections live,
  what to check first if it breaks
- Short recorded walkthrough video
- No dependency on Stevo for basic day-2 operation — agency can hand the doc to anyone

## White-label / confidentiality boundary

- No Stevo AI Business branding, credit, or mention in anything shown to the agency's client
- NDA on request
- Agency owns the client relationship, pricing, sales, and account management end to end — Stevo
  is the build layer only

# 10 — First $1,000 Plan

Built strictly on the same, now-validated channel. No unvalidated scaling
assumptions (per instruction).

## The mechanism that gets there

Once the first job produces a 5-star review, the market evidence in
01-MARKET-DEMAND.md says the dynamic changes materially: "once you have
2–3 solid 5-star reviews, everything changes." That's the entire growth
lever for this plan — reviews compounding proposal win-rate on the exact
same channel already validated, not a new channel.

## Path

1. **Jobs 1–3 ($150–$450 combined):** Same narrow Make.com fix/finish/QA
   scope. Price stays at $99–$199 — do not raise price yet; the goal is
   review velocity, not margin, until 3 reviews exist.
2. **Reviews 1–3 land:** Proposal win-rate rises (market evidence: this is
   the single biggest lever new freelancers have). No new skill required —
   same scope, same buyer profile.
3. **Jobs 4–7 ($150–$300 each, ~$900–$1,200 combined):** Same scope,
   modestly higher price once 2–3 reviews exist ($150–$250 range), since
   trust signal now exists independent of price.
4. **Total: $1,000+ crossed within roughly 7–10 jobs of this exact type,**
   assuming the 72-hour test's success thresholds hold up over repeated
   windows.

## What does NOT change on the way to $1,000
- No move to deeper technical scope (still bounded by the hard rejection
  filter — MLS, CRM APIs, telephony, custom auth stay out).
- No shift to outbound cold-touch — acquisition stays buyer-initiated.
- No new product line introduced mid-path; the template/SOP packaging
  asset (05 #4) can run in parallel as a zero-marginal-cost side channel
  but isn't counted as part of this $1,000, since its discovery mechanism
  is unvalidated (07).

## What legitimately *can* change once $1,000 is banked
- Modest price increase toward the existing $199 "founding implementation
  price," now backed by real reviews instead of a cold profile.
- Optional reconsideration of ecommerce or a second channel — but only
  funded by banked profit, never by capital risked before this point, per
  03-ECOMMERCE-REALITY-CHECK.md.

## What this plan deliberately does not assume
- It does not assume paid ads work (none used).
- It does not assume a second marketplace (Fiverr) outperforms Upwork —
  it's a backup, not a scaling assumption.
- It does not assume retainer/VA-style work (05 #3) scales the same way —
  that model is time-for-money and is treated as a separate, optional
  track, not folded into this number.

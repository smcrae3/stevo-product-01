# 11 — What to Stop

Each item below is evaluated for opportunity cost, not stopped
automatically, per instruction.

## STOP — broad outbound cold email/cold touches
**Opportunity cost of continuing:** ~20 touches produced 0 replies. The
Phase 1 evidence (uphunt.io) directly explains why: cold strangers have no
reason to trust a zero-review freelancer, while marketplace buyers who
post their own problem are self-selected as already looking. Every hour
spent on outbound is an hour not spent on marketplace bids that the
evidence says convert better for exactly this founder stage. **Stop
immediately.**

## STOP — more dashboards / more websites
**Opportunity cost:** None of the five winning models in 05-TOP-5.md
require a dashboard or a marketing website to close the first sale — the
marketplace *is* the storefront. Time here is pure "tool collection trap"
territory, the exact failure mode Reddit evidence names as the top reason
side hustles produce $0. **Stop.**

## STOP — Product-02 / new SaaS
**Opportunity cost:** Building a second product before the first has a
single paying customer inverts the evidence-first discipline this whole
document is built on. **Stop — explicitly ruled out already in the
existing Astra strategy, and this research confirms it independently.**

## STOP — more automation demos (as a standalone activity)
**Opportunity cost:** A demo with no attached buyer produces the same
zero signal a portfolio piece does without a client behind it. The
existing Trades Lead Response build is *proof enough* — it doesn't need a
sibling. **Stop building new demos; reuse the one that exists** (see
05-TOP-5.md #2 and #4).

## STOP — Shopify setup / dropshipping exploration for now
**Opportunity cost:** Real, per 03-ECOMMERCE-REALITY-CHECK.md — but not
because it's fake. It's capital and time that could instead fund a tested,
already-converting channel. Revisit only after $1,000 in banked service
revenue provides a testing budget Steven can afford to lose.

## PAUSE, don't stop — AI tool collecting
**Opportunity cost:** Named directly in the Reddit evidence as the
single most common reason beginners spend $300–$500/month for zero
income. Steven already has Claude, ChatGPT/Codex, and Gemini/Antigravity —
sufficient tooling exists. No new subscriptions until a tool solves a
problem an existing one demonstrably can't.

## DO NOT automatically stop — certifications (AWS AI Practitioner,
PL-900, Power Automate)
**Opportunity cost analysis, not a blanket stop:** These have a different
payoff shape than the business experiment — they de-risk the "Career"
side of the Phase 17 decision (steady income, employer-recognized
credential) rather than competing directly for the same hours as
marketplace bidding, which mostly needs short, async bursts (proposal
writing, scenario testing) that don't conflict with evening study time the
same way a demo-building spree would. See 12-CAREER-VS-BUSINESS.md for the
full comparison — the answer there is "keep both, sequenced," not "drop
one."

## DO NOT stop — content or research entirely
**Opportunity cost analysis:** Broad, unfocused "content" and "research"
as open-ended activities should stop; *targeted* research (reading the
actual task text of a live job before bidding, scoring a candidate against
the instrument) is core to the winning model and should continue. The
distinction is scope, not the activity itself.

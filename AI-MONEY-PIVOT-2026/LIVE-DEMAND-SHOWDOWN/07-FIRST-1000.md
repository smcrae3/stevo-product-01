# 07 — First-$1,000 Test, Per Lane

No scaling assumptions beyond what repeating the same validated first-$100
motion implies, per instruction.

## Lane A — Automation repair

- ~7–10 transactions at $99–$199 average (price rising modestly as reviews
  accumulate, per the existing evidence that 2–3 reviews change win rate).
- Same channel, same scope discipline, no new skill required.
- Realistic given confirmed category-level pricing and demand.

## Lane B — Subcontracting

- Cannot be modeled. No confirmed transaction count is possible without
  first solving the discoverability problem in 02/05. Any number given
  here would be invented, which the mission explicitly forbids.

## Lane C — Nontechnical AI services

- ~15–25 transactions at $40–$70 average (lower per-unit price than A,
  per confirmed category rate evidence), OR a smaller number of retainer-
  style VA arrangements ($200–$400/month) if Steven shifts from one-off
  cleanup jobs to an ongoing relationship after 2–3 good deliveries.
- Realistic, but requires roughly double the transaction volume of Lane A
  to hit the same revenue target, which matters directly for a founder
  with limited weekly hours.

## Reading

At $1,000, the transaction-count gap between A and C becomes the
deciding practical factor: A needs about half as many client
relationships to reach the same number, which matters more as Steven's
available hours (full-time job + career study) are the actual constraint,
not the marketing effort per job.

## Why this doesn't shift even though the flagship opportunity failed

The prior career-vs-business analysis was never built on the assumption
that any single job would succeed — it was built on the shape of the
work (short-burst, async, fits in pockets of time that don't compete with
scheduled cert study) versus a full pivot. That reasoning is untouched by
one job turning out to be a bad candidate. If anything, this correction
reinforces PAUSE-BUSINESS being the wrong call: the fix for a bad
candidate is a five-minute verification check, not abandoning the lane or
escalating founder hours into it.

  conceptually close to Power Automate and to the "Make/API fundamentals"

  it further would cost real hours (searching for a relationship-based
  door) for a lane already eliminated in 08-WINNER.md. Not recommended

# 06 — First-$100 Test, Per Lane

## Lane A — Automation repair

- **Exact job type:** Bounded Sheets/Zapier/Make mapping, routing, or QA
  fix — verified complete (all tasks readable) before bidding.
- **Typical price:** $75–$250 fixed.
- **Sales needed:** 1.
- **Buyer source:** Upwork/Fiverr fixed-price listings, buyer-posted.
- **Steven does:** Reads full listing personally before bidding (standing
  rule now), confirms scope, tests the result, writes plain-English
  summary, communicates directly.
- **AI does:** Diagnosis drafting, fix proposals, test-case generation,
  proposal copy.
- **Biggest failure risk:** Bidding on an incomplete or unclear listing
  again — mitigated only by Steven personally reading the full post before
  every bid, no exceptions.

## Lane B — Subcontracting

- **Exact job type:** Undetermined — no accessible entry-level listing
  type was found.
- **Typical price:** Unknown from available evidence.
- **Sales needed:** N/A — no verified path to a first transaction.
- **Buyer source:** Would require a direct relationship not currently
  reachable via any demand-capture channel found.
- **Steven does / AI does:** Cannot be specified responsibly without a
  real entry point.
- **Biggest failure risk:** The lane itself may not produce a first sale
  within any reasonable timeframe — this is the disqualifying finding.

## Lane C — Nontechnical AI services

- **Exact job type:** Presentation/spreadsheet cleanup or a short research
  brief — chosen over generic VA data entry because it has the least
  global-price-floor competition of the category (cleanup/research need a
  specific short deliverable, not ongoing cheap hourly labor).
- **Typical price:** $30–$100 fixed for a bounded cleanup/brief.
- **Sales needed:** 1–2.
- **Buyer source:** Upwork fixed-price listings, buyer-posted.
- **Steven does:** Confirms exact deliverable format upfront, does the
  actual review/quality pass on AI's first draft, communicates plainly.
- **AI does:** Drafts the cleanup, structures the research, first-pass
  formatting.
- **Biggest failure risk:** Underpricing against ultra-low-cost
  competitors, or over-promising turnaround speed competitors already
  advertise (12–24 hr) without verifying feasibility first.

## Comparative read

A and C both have a credible, same-scale path to $100 in 1–2 transactions.
B does not have a credible path at all on current evidence — this alone
is close to disqualifying for the $100 milestone specifically, independent
of the demand-level analysis in 02.

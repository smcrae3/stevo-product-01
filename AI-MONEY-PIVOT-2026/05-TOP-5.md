# 05 — Top 5 Current Options

## 1. Marketplace-native Make.com fix/finish/QA gigs (narrow scope)

- **Exact thing sold:** Diagnosing, finishing, and stress-testing a
  DIY-built Make.com scenario. Not "automation consulting" — a bounded,
  named repair.
- **Target buyer:** First-time or low-history Upwork/Fiverr client, solo
  or small-business owner, non-technical, who built something themselves
  (often with AI help) and got stuck.
- **Where buyer is found:** Upwork (fixed-price, Make.com/Zapier
  keyword), Fiverr gig discovery.
- **Typical price:** $75–$250 fixed.
- **Est. gross margin:** ~90%+ (time cost only, AI-assisted).
- **Startup cost:** $0–$15 (Connects).
- **Time to first market test:** Same day.
- **AI does:** Reads the scenario logic, drafts diagnosis, proposes fixes,
  generates test cases, drafts client-facing summaries.
- **Steven does:** Confirms scope before committing, makes the actual
  judgment calls on what's "responsibly deliverable," runs and watches the
  live test, communicates plainly with the client, says "I don't know, let
  me check" when true.
- **Biggest failure mode:** Underestimating a task's real complexity before
  seeing the scenario, then being stuck mid-job.
- **Why it could work:** Direct evidence match — real live job already
  found with this exact profile; matches the market's own advice for
  beginners (small fixed scope, first-time client, reviews are the prize).
- **Why it could fail:** 16+ proposals per job means real competition even
  at this size; a first bad review is costly with zero track record.

## 2. AI-assisted lead-response / lead-routing builds for local service
businesses

- **Exact thing sold:** A Forms/Sheets/Gmail (or CRM-adjacent, no-code)
  automation that gets a lead answered fast.
- **Target buyer:** Trades and local service businesses losing leads to
  slow response.
- **Where buyer is found:** Upwork/Fiverr search; secondarily, Make
  Community threads where a business owner describes this exact pain.
- **Typical price:** $150–$300 fixed, matches existing $199 offer.
- **Est. gross margin:** ~85%.
- **Startup cost:** $0–$20.
- **Time to first market test:** 1–3 days.
- **AI does:** Scaffolds the scenario, drafts copy for confirmation
  emails/texts, documentation.
- **Steven does:** Confirms the actual business logic matches how the
  client's leads really flow, tests with realistic bad data, explains it
  in plain English.
- **Biggest failure mode:** Client's actual lead source is messier than
  described (multiple channels, existing partial automation) and scope
  balloons past what was quoted.
- **Why it could work:** This is Steven's existing built asset — it's
  already proof, not a new build.
- **Why it could fail:** Slower buyer discovery than the "already-broken
  scenario" cluster, since it competes with build-from-scratch agencies
  too.

---

## 3. AI-assisted admin/ops support (VA-style, retainer)

- **Exact thing sold:** Ongoing light operations support — inbox triage,
  scheduling, simple reporting, workflow upkeep — for a solo founder or
  small business, AI-assisted.
- **Target buyer:** Solo founders and small teams who can't yet afford a
  full VA but are drowning in repetitive tasks.
- **Where buyer is found:** Upwork (hourly/retainer listings), possibly
  referrals from the automation-fix work above.
- **Typical price:** $20–$40/hr or $200–$500/month retainer.
- **Est. gross margin:** ~70% (more of Steven's actual hours involved).
- **Startup cost:** $0.
- **Time to first market test:** 3–7 days.
- **AI does:** Drafts, summarizes, triages, prepares reports.
- **Steven does:** Judgment calls, actual client communication, execution
  of anything AI can't safely auto-send.
- **Biggest failure mode:** Time-for-money retainer work competes directly
  with the full-time job for Steven's limited hours — lowest leverage of
  the five.
- **Why it could work:** Very low technical bar, plays directly to
  Steven's communication strength, easiest to explain honestly.
- **Why it could fail:** Doesn't scale the way project-based automation
  work does; caps upside.

## 4. Workflow/SOP template packaging (Gumroad/Etsy, secondary asset)

- **Exact thing sold:** A ready-to-use Make.com blueprint or Sheets/Forms
  template, built from Steven's actual working scenarios, sold as a
  digital download.
- **Target buyer:** Solo founders/freelancers who'd rather buy a working
  starting point than build from zero.
- **Where buyer is found:** Gumroad, Etsy digital section — search/browse
  discovery, not outbound.
- **Typical price:** $15–$60 one-time.
- **Est. gross margin:** ~95%+ after first sale (zero marginal cost).
- **Startup cost:** $0.
- **Time to first market test:** 1–2 days to package and list.
- **AI does:** Nearly all production — documentation, listing copy,
  screenshots-to-instructions.
- **Steven does:** Confirms the template genuinely works standalone
  (without his own account context), picks the one asset most likely to
  have a real search audience.
- **Biggest failure mode:** Discovery — no evidence found for how a new,
  zero-review listing gets found; could sit at zero sales indefinitely.
- **Why it could work:** Reuses existing work at zero marginal cost; not
  competing for the same hours as client delivery.
- **Why it could fail:** Passive listing with no active demand-capture
  channel — the weakest "buyer already looking" fit of the five.

## 5. AI content repurposing/editing for solo creators

- **Exact thing sold:** Turning one piece of long-form content into
  multiple short-form/platform-native pieces, AI-assisted.
- **Target buyer:** Solo content creators without a team.
- **Where buyer is found:** Upwork/Fiverr creator-services category.
- **Typical price:** $50–$300/project.
- **Est. gross margin:** ~80%.
- **Startup cost:** $0.
- **Time to first market test:** 2–4 days.
- **AI does:** Most of the actual repurposing/drafting work.
- **Steven does:** Quality judgment, matches creator's voice, client
  communication.
- **Biggest failure mode:** No existing proof asset — unlike the
  automation clusters, Steven has never delivered this before, so the
  first job would be a true cold start with nothing to point to.
- **Why it could work:** Real, growing category; low technical bar.
- **Why it could fail:** Weakest Steven Fit of the five — no track record,
  no natural on-ramp from current assets, would require building a new
  proof sample from scratch before even bidding.

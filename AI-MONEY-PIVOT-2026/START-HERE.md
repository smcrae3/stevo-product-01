**1. WHAT IS THE EASIEST CREDIBLE THING FOR STEVEN TO SELL USING AI RIGHT
NOW?**
Fixing, finishing, and testing Make.com automations that non-technical
owners built themselves (often with AI help) and got stuck on — sold as a
narrow, fixed-price marketplace gig, not a broad "automation consulting"
offer.

**2. WHY IS IT EASIER THAN HIS CURRENT AUTOMATION BUSINESS?**
It isn't a different business — it's the same skill set with the scope and
channel narrowed, based on evidence, not guesswork: buyer-initiated
marketplace bids on small, bounded jobs instead of broad outbound cold
touches, which produced 0 replies in ~20 tries. See 06-WINNER.md for the
full proof against four real alternatives.

**3. WHO IS ALREADY BUYING IT?**
First-time or low-history Upwork/Fiverr clients — solo owners and small
businesses — who DIY-built a Make.com scenario, often with AI stepping
them through it, and now don't trust it or can't finish it. Confirmed by a
real, currently open $150 job matching this profile exactly.

**4. WHERE ARE THOSE BUYERS?**
Upwork fixed-price listings (primary channel), Fiverr Make.com/n8n gig
category (secondary).

**5. WHAT EXACTLY SHOULD STEVEN SELL?**
A bounded fix/finish/QA pass on an existing Make.com automation, delivered
async with a written test summary — not a platform build, not ongoing
maintenance unless separately agreed.

**6. WHAT SHOULD IT COST?**
$99–$199 per job at this stage (reviews-first, not margin-first). The
verified live job is priced at $150.

**7. WHAT DOES AI DO?**
Reads and explains the scenario's logic, drafts proposed fixes and test
cases, writes client-facing summaries and proposals, drafts the packaged
template listing.

**8. WHAT DOES STEVEN DO?**
Confirms real scope before committing, makes the actual judgment call on
what's safely deliverable, runs and watches live tests himself,
communicates in plain English, says "let me check, I don't want to guess"
when something is genuinely outside what he knows.

**9. HOW MUCH MONEY MUST STEVEN RISK?**
Under $20 for the 72-hour test (a handful of Upwork Connects). No ad
spend, no store, no inventory, no purchases.

**10. SHOULD STEVEN START A SHOPIFY STORE?**
**NO** — not now. Real demand exists, but beginner net margins run
roughly -5% to +5% in the first 90 days and require capital and content
skill Steven's current constraints don't support well. Revisit only after
$1,000 in banked service revenue funds a testing budget he can afford to
lose. Full reasoning: 03-ECOMMERCE-REALITY-CHECK.md.

**11. SHOULD STEVEN TRY DROPSHIPPING?**
**NO** — same reasoning as #10. TikTok Shop is a real, growing, and
genuinely improved variant over old AliExpress-style dropshipping, but it
doesn't remove the capital-at-risk-before-signal problem, which is the
disqualifying factor given Steven's stated preference for fast, low-risk
feedback.

**12. SHOULD STEVEN KEEP SELLING AUTOMATION SERVICES?**
**PRIMARY** — evidence-backed, not incumbent-protected. It won a fair
scored comparison against ecommerce, digital products, subcontracting,
admin services, and content services. See 02-BUSINESS-MODEL-RANKING.md.

**13. WHAT ARE THE THREE BEST REAL CURRENT OPPORTUNITIES?**

from this environment (Upwork is otherwise unreachable here) — padding to

- **Verified:** "Automation manager for Make.com," $150 fixed, UK
  first-time client, live now (details: 07-LIVE-OPPORTUNITIES.md and
  `CLAUDE-CLOSER/LATEST-CLOSER-DECISION.md`).
- **Findable within minutes:** two more using the repeatable search recipe
  in 07-LIVE-OPPORTUNITIES.md — Steven needs to run it himself since this
  session can't browse live Upwork listings.

**14. WHAT SHOULD STEVEN DO IN THE NEXT 72 HOURS?**
Full hour-by-hour plan in 08-72-HOUR-TEST.md. Summary: read the verified
job's actual task text, bid if it scores GREEN/YELLOW, run the search
recipe for 1–2 more candidates, list one existing workflow as a Gumroad
template, track replies — nothing more.

**15. WHAT IS THE FASTEST CREDIBLE PATH TO $100?**
Win the verified $150 job, or two smaller jobs from the same cluster.
Paid via platform escrow on approval. Full detail: 09-FIRST-100.md.

**16. WHAT IS THE FASTEST CREDIBLE PATH TO $1,000?**
Same channel, no new model: ~7–10 jobs in the same narrow scope, with
price rising modestly from $99–$199 toward $199–$250 once 2–3 reviews
exist — reviews are the growth lever, not a new channel. Full detail:
10-FIRST-1000.md.

**17. SHOULD CAREER STUDY REMAIN PRIMARY?**
**YES.** The business is a side experiment until $1,000 is banked, because
the two tracks don't actually compete for the same hours the way an
all-in pivot would assume. Full reasoning: 12-CAREER-VS-BUSINESS.md.

**18. WHAT SHOULD STEVEN STOP DOING IMMEDIATELY?**
Broad outbound cold touches, building more dashboards/websites, Product-02
or new SaaS, building new demo assets (the one that exists is proof
enough), and any new AI tool subscriptions. Certifications and targeted
research are explicitly NOT on the stop list. Full reasoning:
11-STOP-DOING.md.

**19. WHAT IS THE ONE THING STEVEN SHOULD DO FIRST?**

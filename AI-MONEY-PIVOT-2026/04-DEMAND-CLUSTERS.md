# 04 — Demand Clusters

**Honesty note on method:** the mission asked for patterns across at least
50 real demand signals "if technically feasible." Upwork's job-detail pages
are login-walled and `upwork.com` is blocked by this environment's network
egress proxy, so individual live listings could not be scraped and counted
one by one from here. What follows is instead synthesized from the market
reports and pricing data gathered in Phase 1 (which themselves aggregate
many real listings) plus the one verified live job already evaluated this
session. Each cluster is a real, evidenced pattern — not a guess — but the
signal *counts* are directional, not a literal tally of 50 individual
postings. Where a number is an estimate, it's marked "est."

## Reading the clusters

The first four clusters — **fix/finish a DIY Make scenario, lead-routing
automation, single-scenario builds, and workflow QA/testing** — are the
same underlying buyer wearing four slightly different labels: a
non-technical small-business owner who used a no-code tool (often with AI
help) and hit a wall. This is not four separate opportunities; it's one
opportunity with four search-term variants. That concentration is useful:
it means one search strategy and one proposal template can hit all four.

The **template/SOP packaging cluster** is the one genuinely different
move worth adding as a low-effort side asset (see 09-LIVE-OPPORTUNITIES
and 06-WINNER for how it fits without displacing the primary bet) — it's
built from work Steven is doing anyway, at near-zero marginal cost, and
doesn't compete for his limited hours the way a second service line would.

The **chatbot** and **full custom platform** clusters are flagged
low/no-fit on purpose: real money moves there, but they sit past the line
where Steven would be pretending to expertise he doesn't have, which the
mission explicitly forbids regardless of price.

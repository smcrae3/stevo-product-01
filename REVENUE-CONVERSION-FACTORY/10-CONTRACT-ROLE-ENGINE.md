# 10 — CONTRACT ROLE ENGINE

**Why this engine matters more than it feels like it does:** applications are
**free**, the rates are the highest confirmed numbers in this entire pack,
and one landed contract ends the freelance scramble. Steven's failure mode
here is **under-applying**, not over-applying.

**Capacity:** 1-2 hrs/week → **5 applications per weekday, 45 minutes,
AI-tailored, Steven reads before sending.**

**Universal rule: apply at 70% match.** Job descriptions are wish lists.
Every role family below has a "what he lacks" column — that is honesty for
Steven's planning, **not** a reason to skip the application.

---

## FAMILY 1 — PROCESS DOCUMENTATION / SOP SPECIALIST ⭐ HIGHEST PRIORITY

**WHY STEVEN FITS.** This is his A-grade cluster with a job title attached:
interview the person who does the work, understand it, write it so someone
else can follow it. Years of explaining complicated things to frustrated
people is the harder version of the same skill.

**WHAT HE LACKS.** The job title on a résumé; enterprise or regulated
documentation experience; formal technical-writing tools (MadCap, Confluence
at scale). **Mitigation:** the P3 sample SOP closes most of this gap in one
click.

**RATE.** **$28.85-$47.12/hr** (US avg $38.94). Ask band: **$30-38/hr.**
Do not anchor at $20 — the published data supports the higher number and a
low ask signals inexperience.

**SEARCH TERMS.** `SOP writer` · `process documentation specialist` ·
`standard operating procedures` · `documentation specialist remote` ·
`process writer` · `work instruction writer` · `knowledge base writer` ·
`training documentation`

**SOURCES.** ZipRecruiter (best rate data), Indeed, LinkedIn, Robert Half,
Beacon Hill, We Work Remotely.

**RÉSUMÉ EMPHASIS.** Process documentation, requirements gathering, training
material creation, quality review, cross-functional communication. Lead with
the sample link.

**INTERVIEW RISK.** *"Walk me through documenting a process you know nothing
about."* Answer in `16`. Second risk: being asked about a specific
documentation platform — answer honestly, note that tools are learnable and
the interviewing skill isn't.

**CAREER VALUE.** **10/10.** Directly on the path to business analysis.

## FAMILY 2 — OPERATIONS ASSISTANT / COORDINATOR

**WHY STEVEN FITS.** Organizing messy information, following through,
communicating with people who are annoyed. Direct T-Mobile transfer.

**WHAT HE LACKS.** Named ops-tool experience (NetSuite, Monday, Asana at
scale), and the title.

**RATE.** **$15.38-$22.36/hr** (avg $19.82). Ask: **$20-24/hr.**

**SEARCH TERMS.** `operations assistant remote` · `operations coordinator
part time` · `business operations assistant` · `admin operations remote` ·
`ops associate`

**SOURCES.** Indeed (highest volume), ZipRecruiter, Glassdoor, LinkedIn,
Robert Half, Insight Global.

**RÉSUMÉ EMPHASIS.** Customer operations, process adherence, escalation
handling, reporting, Google Workspace.

**INTERVIEW RISK.** Being screened as "just a CSR." **Mitigation:** lead
every answer with a *systems* observation, not a service one — "I noticed
the same escalation came in 40 times a month, so I…" rather than "I helped
customers."

**CAREER VALUE.** 8/10 — the standard on-ramp.

## FAMILY 3 — IMPLEMENTATION COORDINATOR / ONBOARDING SPECIALIST

**WHY STEVEN FITS.** Customer-facing plus process-facing simultaneously —
an unusual combination and exactly his shape. Requirements gathering,
explaining systems to non-technical users, keeping a rollout on track.

**WHAT HE LACKS.** SaaS implementation experience; a specific platform
(Salesforce, HubSpot, NetSuite).

**RATE.** ~$20-30/hr contract; $45-65K salaried equivalent.

**SEARCH TERMS.** `implementation coordinator` · `implementation specialist` ·
`onboarding specialist` · `client onboarding coordinator` ·
`implementation support` · `deployment coordinator`

**SOURCES.** Wellfound, LinkedIn, Indeed, SaaS company career pages directly.

**RÉSUMÉ EMPHASIS.** Customer onboarding, requirements gathering, training
delivery, documentation, escalation management.

**INTERVIEW RISK.** *"Have you implemented [platform]?"* Answer honestly:
"Not that one. I've learned Make.com, Apps Script, and Sheets workflows, and
what I'm actually good at is the part where you sit with a customer and
figure out what they need before anyone configures anything."

**CAREER VALUE.** **10/10** — the single best bridge role in this list.

## FAMILY 4 — AI EVALUATOR / QA ANALYST

**WHY STEVEN FITS.** Rubric adherence, consistent judgment, clear written
reasoning, catching what's subtly wrong. Overlaps Engine 1 entirely, but as
a *contract role* it comes with stable hours instead of volatile throughput —
which is Engine 1's main weakness.

**WHAT HE LACKS.** ML background (not required at this tier); formal QA
tooling (Jira, TestRail) for software-QA variants.

**RATE.** $17-30/hr generalist; $30-55/hr RLHF; software QA analyst $25-40/hr.

**SEARCH TERMS.** `AI evaluator` · `AI trainer` · `model evaluation` ·
`content quality analyst` · `QA analyst remote contract` ·
`quality assurance specialist` · `search quality rater`

**SOURCES.** The 22 platforms in `02`, plus LinkedIn and Indeed for direct
in-house roles at AI companies.

**RÉSUMÉ EMPHASIS.** Quality review, rubric application, consistency,
written analysis, attention to detail.

**INTERVIEW RISK.** Low. Assessments are the interview.

**CAREER VALUE.** 7/10 — current and marketable, but shallower than
implementation or BA work.

## FAMILY 5 — SPREADSHEET / GOOGLE SHEETS SPECIALIST

**WHY STEVEN FITS.** Grade-B-but-real, and he already bid $28/hr on exactly
this shape of work.

**WHAT HE LACKS.** Advanced Apps Script unaided; SQL; BI tooling; VBA at
depth. **Be honest about all four in interviews** — this family punishes
bluffing faster than any other, because they will test him.

**RATE.** $25-40/hr. Steven's own live data point: **$28/hr**, which is a
reasonable anchor.

**SEARCH TERMS.** `google sheets specialist` · `spreadsheet specialist` ·
`excel google sheets contractor` · `data operations associate` ·
`reporting analyst junior` · `google workspace specialist`

**SOURCES.** Upwork (**hourly only**), Indeed, ZipRecruiter, Contra,
staffing firms.

**RÉSUMÉ EMPHASIS.** Data cleanup, normalization, dedupe logic, reporting,
Forms/Sheets workflow design, Apps Script **with AI assistance** — stated
that way.

**INTERVIEW RISK.** **A live technical test.** Real possibility. Steven
should practice XLOOKUP, QUERY, ARRAYFORMULA, and pivot tables cold, without
AI, before interviewing in this family.

**CAREER VALUE.** 6/10 — useful, but a plateau unless it leads into analytics.

## FAMILY 7 — CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE / CUSTOMER SUCCESS OPERATIONS

**WHY STEVEN FITS.** **This is where his actual résumé is strongest and
where he needs the least reframing.** Retention conversations, escalation
handling, CS quality review, policy interpretation — all real, all unaided,
all professional.

**WHAT HE LACKS.** CS-platform admin experience (Zendesk, Gainsight,
Salesforce Service Cloud); reporting/analytics depth.

**RATE.** $20-32/hr contract.

**SEARCH TERMS.** `customer experience operations` · `customer success
operations` · `CX analyst` · `support operations` · `customer insights
analyst` · `CS quality analyst`

**SOURCES.** LinkedIn, Wellfound, Indeed, SaaS career pages.

**RÉSUMÉ EMPHASIS.** Retention outcomes, quality review, escalation
resolution, voice-of-customer analysis, process improvement.

**INTERVIEW RISK.** Lowest of any family — he can speak from direct
experience without qualification.

**CAREER VALUE.** 7/10 — and it is the **highest-probability first offer**,
because it is the only family where his existing résumé needs no translation.

## FAMILY 8 — JUNIOR BUSINESS ANALYST

**WHY STEVEN FITS.** Requirements gathering, process mapping, stakeholder
communication.

**WHAT HE LACKS.** SQL, formal BA methodology (BABOK), the title, and often
a degree requirement. **This is a 12-month target, not a September one.**

**RATE.** $30-50/hr contract when accessible.

**SEARCH TERMS.** `junior business analyst` · `business analyst contract
remote` · `business systems analyst` · `process analyst`

**SOURCES.** Robert Half, Insight Global, Kforce, Beacon Hill, LinkedIn.

**RÉSUMÉ EMPHASIS.** Requirements, process documentation, stakeholder
management, data analysis.

**INTERVIEW RISK.** **High** — likely SQL screening and methodology
questions. Apply anyway; a contract-to-hire BA role is how most people enter
this field, and staffing firms place at entry level.

**CAREER VALUE.** **10/10** — this is the destination.

## CALIBRATION — so silence doesn't get misread
A **2-5% application-to-interview rate is normal.** Twenty silent
applications is not failure. **Two hundred** silent applications means the
résumé or the targeting is wrong — fix the input, don't just add volume.

**The staffing-firm shortcut:** one good conversation with a Robert Half or
Insight Global recruiter can produce placements for years, because **the
recruiter does the acquisition.** That is worth more than fifty cold
applications and Steven should make those calls in week 2.

# 18 — 30-DAY EXECUTION CALENDAR

## THE CAPACITY BUDGET — read this before the calendar

**Steven has a full-time job.** The realistic budget:

| | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon-Fri** | **60-90 min/day** | One 90-minute Money Block (`19`), or a shortened 45-min version on hard days |
| **Sat** | **2-3 hrs** | Deep block — proof building, agency research |
| **Sun** | **1-2 hrs** | Deep block + 20-min weekly review |
| **TOTAL** | **9-12 hrs/week** | |

**This is a ceiling, not a target.** Some weeks will be 6 hours. That is
fine and the plan survives it — because nothing here compounds only if done
daily.

**Two protections built in:**
- **One full rest day per week.** Non-negotiable. Burnout at week 3 costs
  more than the hours it saves.
- **Every week has at most 7 distinct commitments.** If a week feels
  overloaded, cut the contract applications first — they are the most
  resilient to interruption.

## THE RULES THAT PROTECT THE PLAN

1. **Never skip a Sunday review.** It is the only mechanism that reallocates
   hours toward what's working. Skipping it turns a plan into a habit.
2. **One rest day per week. Genuinely off.**
3. **If a week goes badly, cut contract applications first** — they are the
   most interruption-tolerant.
4. **Do not add anything to this calendar.** Not YouTube, not a new tool, not
   a course. The calendar is already at capacity.
5. **Do not spend Connects.** Not one, until an engine produces income.
6. **Proof pieces before outreach.** Never message an agency before P3 and
   P4 exist — an unproven cold pitch burns a target permanently.

## WHAT IS DELIBERATELY ABSENT
- **YouTube / content.** Zero hours for 30 days. It cannot produce September
  money and every hour it takes comes from something that can.
- **Learning n8n, SQL, or any new platform.** Skill-building is week 6+,
  after cash exists.
- **Perfecting anything.** Shipped and adequate beats polished and late,
  in all four engines.

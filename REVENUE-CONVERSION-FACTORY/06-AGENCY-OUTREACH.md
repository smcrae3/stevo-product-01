# 06 — AGENCY OUTREACH PACK

## THE RULES
1. **Never lead with "I run an AI business."** Agency owners get twenty of
   those a week and delete all of them. Lead with **bounded backend
   execution**: documentation, QA, research, spreadsheet and process support.
2. **Never claim a client Steven has not served.** No fake case studies, no
   "clients like yours," no implied roster. Samples are labeled
   **DEMONSTRATION**, and that honesty is a selling point, not a weakness.
3. **One specific, real detail about them per message.** No detail → don't send.
4. **Ask for something small.** A $200 pilot, not a partnership.
5. **State the exit.** "If it's not a fit, no follow-up from me." It lowers
   the cost of reading and raises reply rate.
6. **Never send twice in one day, never more than two follow-ups.**

check every A-tier target for a mutual connection on LinkedIn before sending
cold.** Five minutes per target, and it changes the odds materially.

## FOLLOW-UP #1 (send 5-7 days later)

> Hi [Name] — following up once on the note below.
>
> One thing I should have led with: the easiest way to test whether I'm
> useful is a **QA pass on your next build before it ships**. $150, 48
> hours, and if I don't find anything worth fixing you don't pay — which is
> a good outcome for you either way.
>
> No pressure, and I won't chase beyond this.
>
> Steven

*Adds new information (a smaller, safer offer) instead of repeating. A
follow-up that just says "just checking in" trains people to ignore you.*

---

## FOLLOW-UP #2 (send 10-14 days after #1 — the last contact, ever)

> Hi [Name] — last note from me, then I'll leave you alone.
>
> If backend production isn't a need right now, that's completely fine. If
> it becomes one — a deadline compresses, someone's out, a client asks for
> documentation you don't have — I turn a handover packet around in 48 hours.
>
> Samples stay live here: [link]. Happy to be a name in your back pocket.
>
> All the best with [specific thing they're working on],
> Steven

**Then stop. Permanently.** Three touches maximum. The "name in your back
pocket" close converts weeks later more often than people expect, and it
costs nothing to leave the door open politely.

reply_type · next_action · follow_up_1_date · follow_up_2_date · outcome`

## READ-THE-RESULTS RULES
| Signal | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0 replies from 10 | Copy or targeting problem | **Rewrite before sending more** |
| 1-2 replies from 10 | **Normal.** Working. | Continue |
| 3+ replies from 10 | Strong | Scale this source |
| Replies but no calls | Offer is unclear or price is wrong | Lead with the $150 QA pilot |
| Calls but no close | Positioning or trust gap | Tighten the boundary line; offer the money-back pilot |

**Do not send 40 messages before reading the first 10.** At 2-3 hrs/week
Steven cannot afford to burn a whole target list on a message that doesn't
work.

## THE THREE SENTENCES THAT DO THE MOST WORK
1. *"I'm not your build engineer and I won't pretend to be."* — disarms the
   objection before it's raised.
2. *"Both are demonstrations, not client work."* — honesty that reads as
   confidence.
3. *"If it's not a fit, no follow-up from me."* — makes the message cheap to
   read, which is the only reason it gets read.

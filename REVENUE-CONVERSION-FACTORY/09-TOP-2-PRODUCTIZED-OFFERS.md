**INPUT REQUIRED (from the buyer — total effort: ~35 minutes).**

   *(Loom, Zoom, or phone video — quality doesn't matter.)*
2. Any existing notes, checklists, or half-finished docs.
3. Answers to five intake questions (below).
4. Confirmation of who the SOP is written *for* — new hire, VA, or client.

**FINAL DELIVERABLE.** A 2-4 page SOP, delivered as PDF **and** editable Google Doc:
- Purpose — what this process achieves and why it matters
- When to use it / when not to
- Prerequisites — access, tools, permissions
- Numbered steps with annotated screenshots
- **Exceptions and edge cases** *(the section that makes it worth $250)*

- Version, date, and change log

**INTAKE FORM (5 questions — no more).**
1. What is the process, in one sentence?
2. Who will read this — a new hire, a VA, a client, or your future self?
3. What goes wrong most often when this process is done badly?
4. What would you tell someone on day one that isn't written anywhere?
5. Is there anything I should *not* include (credentials, customer data,
   pricing)?

*Questions 3 and 4 are the whole engine.* They produce the exceptions
section, which is the difference between a $250 SOP and a transcript.

**"Do you use AI?"** Yes, for transcription and the first draft. Then I walk

— an unverified AI draft is worth nothing and I won't send you one.
**"What if I don't like it?"** One revision is included. On the founding
price, if it's not usable, don't pay.

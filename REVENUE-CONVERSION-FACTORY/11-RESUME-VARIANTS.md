- **Nothing fabricated.** No invented employers, titles, dates, or metrics.
- **No certifications not earned.** No "Google Certified", no "PMP", nothing.
- **AI assistance stated where it's material.** "Apps Script (with AI
  assistance)" is honest and, in 2026, unremarkable. Claiming unaided
  developer skill is the one lie that gets caught in a technical screen.
- **`[BRACKETS]` = Steven fills in.** I don't know his exact tenure, titles,
  or numbers, and I will not invent them. **Every metric must be one he can
  defend if asked "how did you arrive at that?"** — an honest estimate he can
  explain beats a precise number he can't.

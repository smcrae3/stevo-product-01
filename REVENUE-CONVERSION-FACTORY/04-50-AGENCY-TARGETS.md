# 04 — 50 AGENCY TARGETS

## READ THIS BEFORE USING THIS FILE

I will not invent agency names or URLs. A fabricated target with a plausible
URL is worse than no target: Steven burns an outreach hour, and if he ever
references a "case study" that doesn't exist, he loses credibility on first
contact — the one thing Engine 2 cannot survive losing.

So this file is built in two parts:

- **PART A — 12 NAMED ANCHORS.** Real companies, verified to exist via live
  research this session. Steven can research and approach these today.
- **PART B — THE HARVEST ENGINE.** 38 slots with the exact directory, exact
  filter, and exact scoring rubric to fill them. Antigravity mission **AG-3**
  executes this and returns a completed `AGENCY-TARGETS.csv`.

Part B is not a placeholder for missing work. The named directories in it
contain **thousands** of qualifying agencies, and which 38 are right depends
on who is visibly busy *this week* — a thing that can only be checked live.

---

## THE SCORING RUBRIC (/100) — apply to every target

| Dimension | Pts | What earns full marks |
|---|---|---|
| **Size fit** | 20 | 2-15 people. Big enough to have a delivery tail, small enough that the owner decides alone. 1-person = 12; 16-40 = 10; 40+ = 0. |
| **Visible delivery load** | 20 | Recent case studies, shipped builds, active posting, or a public client roster. Evidence they are *busy*. |
| **Doc/QA gap signal** | 15 | Thin or absent documentation on their own site; support-burden complaints; "we move fast" positioning. |
| **Contactability** | 15 | Named owner, findable email or active LinkedIn. A generic `info@` form scores 5. |
| **Subcontractor precedent** | 10 | Has hired freelancers, posted for help, or advertises a partner network. |
| **Rate capacity** | 10 | US/EU/AU client base and premium positioning. Offshore-priced shops score 0 — they cannot pay $25-40/hr. |
| **Personalization surface** | 10 | Something specific and real to reference: a named build, a post, a talk. |

**A-tier ≥ 75** (contact in week 1-2) · **B-tier 55-74** (weeks 3-4) ·
**C-tier < 55** (do not contact).

**Hard disqualifiers, regardless of score:** 50+ employees · offshore
low-cost positioning · no findable human · dormant (no activity in 12
months) · they sell the exact service Steven sells (competitor, not client).

### What to capture per slot (this is the CSV schema)
`agency_name, url, niche, size_signal, services, why_steven_fits,
specific_backend_task (M1-M18), contact_name, contact_role, contact_route,
personalization_angle, likely_pay_model, risk, score_/100, tier`

### The personalization requirement — the thing that decides reply rate
Every row must carry **one specific, real, verifiable detail**: a named
build, a case study, a post they wrote, a tool they specialize in, a talk
they gave.

> "I saw your Make Gold partner work" is generic and will be ignored.
> "I saw the inventory-sync build you wrote up for [client] — that's the
> kind of thing where the handoff doc usually gets written last" is specific
> and gets a reply.

If a row has no personalization angle, **drop the row**. A target you cannot
personalize is not a target.

## OUTREACH SEQUENCING (10 per week, not 50 at once)

| Week | Targets | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 | Part A anchors 1-3 + 7 Make-directory harvests |
| 2 | 10 | Zapier Experts + n8n Jobs board |
| 3 | 10 | Airtable + HubSpot + Reddit |
| 4 | 10 | LinkedIn + Upwork-inverted + Part A remainder |
| 5 | 10 | Best-performing source from weeks 1-4 |

**Why not all 50 at once:** at 2-3 hrs/week Steven cannot research 50
properly, and an unpersonalized message is worse than no message. **Ten
researched beat a hundred generic.**

**The feedback rule:** if week 1's ten produce **zero replies**, do not send
week 2's ten. Rewrite the message first. Zero from ten is a copy or
targeting problem, and sending forty more of the same is how a channel gets
falsely written off.

## WHAT NOT TO DO
- Do not contact all 12 anchors in one day. Ten per week, with real research.
- Do not approach any agency's clients. Ever.
- Do not send before the two proof pieces in `07` exist.
- Do not pitch "I run an AI business." Pitch bounded backend execution.

# 20 — 7-DAY CASH SPRINT

**GOAL: maximize the probability of the first new dollar.**

**The honest framing:** the highest-probability *cash-in-hand-this-week* path
is the **warm network** — a $95 Sheet Rescue or a $150 SOP for someone who
already trusts Steven. The highest-probability *first-real-income* path is
**AI evaluation**, which pays in 2-5 weeks but is nearly certain to pay
eventually.

**So the sprint runs both**, and everything else is secondary. Nothing in
these seven days costs a dollar or a Connect.

## THE SPRINT RULES

1. **Nothing costs money. No Connects. No subscriptions. No courses.**
2. **Proof pieces before agency outreach.** Day 5 depends on Days 2-3.
   Don't reorder.
3. **Replies outrank everything.** A live conversation beats the day's plan —
   drop the plan and take the conversation.
4. **12 minutes per application, maximum.**
5. **If a day is missed, do not double up.** Continue from where the sprint
   is. Doubling up is how people quit on day 4.
6. **Do not check for replies more than twice a day.** Refreshing an inbox is
   not work and it feels exactly like work, which is what makes it dangerous.

## THE STOP CONDITION FOR THE WHOLE SPRINT
If by end of Day 7 Steven has **not** applied to at least 4 AI eval platforms
and built at least 1 proof piece, **stop and do only those two things** in
week 2. Everything else in this pack depends on them.

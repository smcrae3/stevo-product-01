## THE ACQUISITION CAVEAT — READ THIS BEFORE GETTING EXCITED

These delivery economics ($40-95/hr) are real **and they are only half the
equation.**

A $150 job delivered in 2 hours is $75/hr — until you add ten proposals to
win it, at which point it is ~$24/hr. And with **14 Connects**, Steven
cannot buy ten proposals. He can buy two.

> **Productized services are a delivery format, not an acquisition strategy.**

Route them, in this order:
1. **Warm network** — highest conversion, zero cost, the only realistic path
   to cash *this week* (`24`)
2. **Local CSRA** — one bounded experiment, for the case study (`23`)
3. **Agencies** — as Offer 3 inside Engine 2 (`05`)
4. **Upwork Project Catalog** — fixed-scope listings that cost **0 Connects**
5. **Fiverr** — free to list, works in the background forever

**Do not run these cold through Upwork proposals at 14 Connects.** That is
the exact mistake the four submitted proposals already tested.

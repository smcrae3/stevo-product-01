# 05 — THE THREE AGENCY OFFERS

Three offers, one page each. Steven leads with **Offer 1** in outreach
(biggest felt pain), but closes first on **Offer 2** (easiest yes, zero
liability). Offer 3 is the volume filler.

---

# OFFER 1 — DOCUMENTATION / HANDOFF / SOP PRODUCTION

**ONE-SENTENCE PITCH**
> I write the client handover documentation your team never has time for —
> white-labeled, on your template, in 48-72 hours.

**WHO IT IS FOR**
2-15 person automation, no-code, and ops agencies that ship builds faster
than they document them. Best signal: recent case studies, thin public docs,
or a founder who mentions delivery load.

**WHAT IS INCLUDED**
- 30-minute recorded walkthrough with the builder
- 4-8 page handover packet: what was built, how it works, how to use it,
  what to do when it breaks, who to contact
- Annotated screenshots of every key step
- Written on **your** template, in **your** voice, under **your** brand
- One revision round
- A short "what I couldn't verify" note — flagged, never papered over

**WHAT IS NOT INCLUDED**
- Modifying, fixing, or extending the build
- Direct contact with your client unless you arrange it
- Video or LMS production
- Ongoing support or on-call
- Anything I cannot personally inspect and verify

**TURNAROUND** 48-72 hours from the walkthrough. Rush (24 hr): +50%.

**RATE**
$250-400 per build documented, or **$30-40/hr**.
Multi-build: 5 packets for $1,500 (a 25% volume discount that buys
predictable work).

**WHEN HOURLY IS BETTER**
- The build is large or you're not sure how complex it is
- Documentation spans multiple systems
- You want ongoing documentation across several projects
- **Anything where "done" isn't yet defined** — then hourly, always

**EXAMPLE OUTPUT**
A 6-page packet for a Make.com lead-routing automation: system overview
diagram, the 4 workflows and their triggers, how to add a new routing rule,
the 5 most likely failure modes with recovery steps, an escalation table,
and a version/change log.

**PROOF PLAN**
Sample SOP from `07`, labeled **DEMONSTRATION — NOT CLIENT WORK**.
Real process, real screenshots, no invented client.

**LOW-RISK PILOT**
> **One build, $200, 72 hours.** You send a walkthrough recording and access.
> If the packet isn't something you'd put your logo on, don't pay me and keep
> the draft.

That last clause converts skeptics, and Steven should mean it. He will
almost never have to honor it, and offering it costs nothing but nerve.

# OFFER 2 — QA + WORKFLOW TESTING + CLIENT-READY CLEANUP

**ONE-SENTENCE PITCH**
> I test your build before your client does, and hand you a bug sheet with
> reproduction steps — so nothing embarrassing ships on a Friday.

**WHO IT IS FOR**
Agencies shipping automations, no-code apps, or integrations without a
dedicated QA person — which is nearly all of them under 15 people.

**WHAT IS INCLUDED**
- Structured test pass against what the build is *supposed* to do
- Happy path, edge cases, and deliberate failure-mode testing
- Bug sheet: issue / severity / **reproduction steps** / expected vs actual /
  screenshot / suggested fix
- Every bug reproduced **twice** before it goes on the sheet
- A reusable regression checklist for future releases
- One re-test pass after your fixes

**WHAT IS NOT INCLUDED**
- **Fixing anything.** I report; your engineer fixes. This boundary is
  permanent.
- Load, security, or penetration testing
- Automated test infrastructure
- Production access without your supervision

**TURNAROUND** 48 hours. Re-test pass: 24 hours.

**RATE**
$150-300 per build, or **$25-35/hr**.
Retainer: $400/month for up to 4 QA passes.

**WHEN HOURLY IS BETTER**
- The system is large or has many integrations
- You want exploratory testing rather than a fixed checklist
- Ongoing QA across releases

**EXAMPLE OUTPUT**
A 14-row bug sheet on a client intake automation: 3 critical (duplicate
records created when the form is double-submitted; confirmation email fires
before the record is written; no handling for a blank required field), 6
medium, 5 cosmetic — each with exact repro steps and a screenshot.

**PROOF PLAN**
Sample QA bug sheet from `07`, on a real workflow Steven deliberately broke.

**LOW-RISK PILOT**
> **One build, $150, 48 hours.** If I find nothing worth fixing, you pay
> nothing — and that's a good outcome for you either way.

**WHY THIS IS THE EASIEST YES**
Zero liability. Steven cannot break anything; he only reports. An owner
weighing "should I let a stranger near my client work" finds this the lowest
possible risk entry point. **Lead with Offer 1 in the email, close on Offer 2
in the call.**

# OFFER 3 — RESEARCH + DATA PREP + SPREADSHEET OPERATIONS

**ONE-SENTENCE PITCH**
> I do the research and data prep your team keeps pushing to Friday —
> sourced, verified, and formatted the way you'd hand it to a client.

**WHO IT IS FOR**
Marketing-ops and research-adjacent agencies, CRM consultants doing
migrations, and any shop that needs verified information at volume.

**WHAT IS INCLUDED**
- Research packs with **every claim cited** and every citation opened and
  verified by a human
- Competitor and market snapshots on your template
- Spreadsheet cleanup, normalization, dedupe — with a change log and a rules
  doc so it stays clean
- Migration prep: field mapping, validation, exceptions log
- Lead-list verification, **priced per verified row**
- Requirements summaries from your call recordings

**WHAT IS NOT INCLUDED**
- **Performing the import or write to a live system.** Prep only — you or
  your client press the button.
- Paywalled or proprietary database access
- Regulated-domain advice (medical, legal, financial)
- Strategic recommendations I can't defend on a call
- Anything sourced from AI without human verification

**TURNAROUND** 48-96 hours depending on volume.

**RATE**
$25-35/hr, or per deliverable: research pack $200-400 · competitor snapshot
$250-400 · cleanup $150-300 · migration prep $300-600 · verification
$175-350.

**WHEN HOURLY IS BETTER — and this is the important one**
- **Any research where the source list is open.** Always hourly. This is
  the exact shape that models out at $11.90/hr fixed.
- Any dataset Steven hasn't seen yet
- Any list where "verified" hasn't been defined in writing
- Any migration where field mapping isn't settled

**EXAMPLE OUTPUT**
A 3,400-row CRM export deduped to 2,890 unique records: phone and state
formats normalized, 190 exceptions logged with reasons, a rules doc so their
VA can maintain it, and a reconciliation showing where all 510 removed rows
went.

**PROOF PLAN**
Source-checked research sample + a before/after spreadsheet cleanup, both
from `07`.

**LOW-RISK PILOT**
> **One research pack or one file, $150-200.** Small enough to approve
> without a meeting.

## THE THREE OFFERS AS ONE LADDER

| Step | Offer | Price | What it proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Offer 2 pilot** — QA pass | $150 | He's careful and finds real things |
| 2 | **Offer 1** — handoff packet | $250 | He can write to their brand |
| 3 | **Offer 3** — research / data | $200-400 | He's reliable at volume |
| 4 | Retainer or standing hours | $400+/mo | He's part of their delivery |

**Most of the money in Engine 2 comes from climbing this ladder inside two
or three accounts — not from finding a fiftieth agency.**

## THE POSITIONING LINE THAT HOLDS ALL THREE TOGETHER
> "I'm not your build engineer and I won't pretend to be. What I'm good at
> is taking what your engineer built and making it explainable, tested, and
> documented. That's a different skill, and it's the one you're short on."

Say this early. It is honest, it defuses the "you're not technical enough"
objection before it is raised, and it is the exact sentence that makes an
agency owner trust the rest of the conversation.
